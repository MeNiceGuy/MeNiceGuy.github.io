Teaming and Subcontracting

The written-work partner behind your program

Boswell Consulting Agency LLC is a Virginia management consulting firm built to be the written-work subcontractor behind a prime contractor or a peer firm. I am Dr. Devon Boswell, DBA, the principal of the firm, and the person who does the work. There is no account team and no handoff.

Programs run on documents: policies, compliance files, analyses, reports, and proposals. That is the side of the work I take. Your team keeps the client relationship and the delivery schedule. I produce the written work product behind it, defined in advance, delivered in writing, and checked against its sources before it reaches you.

How an Engagement Runs

Written intake. Defined deliverable. Verified work.

  • Written intake You send the requirement, the source documents, and the deadline in writing. If the requirement is a regulation, a solicitation section, or a funder template, send that too. The work starts from the source, not from a summary of it.
  • Defined deliverable I scope the work in writing before it starts: what the document is, what it gets checked against, and when it comes back. Scope stays locked because it is written down.
  • Verification file included Every deliverable ships with a verification file listing each requirement it was checked against and the source for it, so your team can cross-reference the work instead of taking my word for it.
  • Asynchronous throughout No standing meetings and no status calls. Questions, drafts, and deliveries move in writing, the record stays exact, and your hours buy documents, not meeting time. Turnaround is fast because the model is built for it.
