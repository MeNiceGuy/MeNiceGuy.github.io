Capability Statement

Boswell Consulting Agency LLC

Boswell Consulting Agency LLC is a Virginia-based management consulting firm specializing in healthcare operations, process improvement, and organizational assessment, led by Dr. Devon Boswell, DBA. The firm helps organizations streamline workflows, strengthen compliance, and improve operational performance through practical, data-informed solutions.

Every engagement is delivered in writing on a fully asynchronous model: the client sends materials and data, the firm returns written findings with implementation guides, keeping scope and the record exact.

Core Capabilities

What the firm does

Operations Assessment and Process Improvement

Comprehensive review of organizational workflows, identification of inefficiencies, and practical corrective plans with defined deliverables and timelines.

Policy and Procedure Development

Gap analysis and development of operational policies, compliance documentation, and standard operating procedures.

Program Evaluation and Data Analysis

Performance metrics design, data analysis, and evaluation reporting using Tableau, Power BI, SQL, and Python, summarized so stakeholders can see what needs focus, what is improving, and what is declining.

Quantitative Research and Organizational Studies

Design and execution of quantitative organizational studies using validated survey instruments, a priori power analysis, and multiple regression with reliability and sensitivity checks. Doctoral research: a quantitative correlational study of innovation adoption among 68 managers of privately owned U.S. home health agencies (Boswell, 2026, Capella DBA capstone).

Training Content Development

Educational materials, onboarding systems, and training toolkits for operational teams, covering licensing, staffing, retention, and financial management.

Past Performance

Recent work

Policies and Procedures Buildout, Virginia Home Care Agency (2026)

Rebuilt a client's 40 documents into a complete 62-document compliance packet in under 24 hours, delivered with a full verification file.

Regulatory and Enrollment Advisory, Nine States (2025 to Present)

Documented guidance for home care and residential care operators across VA, NC, MD, TX, OH, GA, IN, MI, and FL, including a stalled North Carolina Medicaid enrollment approved after the firm's escalation plan, Indiana PathWays waiver billing guidance, and North Carolina site-visit readiness under the October 1, 2026 PCG screening rules.
