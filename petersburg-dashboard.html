Questions this view opens with

Question one

The division absenteeism rate fell again this year. Petersburg High (38.6%) and Pleasants Lane (36.1%) still sit at the top of the division. What do those two schools share that the improving schools do not?

Question two

Four schools carry a CSI identification. Two of them, Cool Spring and Pleasants Lane, are fully accredited at the same time. What evidence is carrying their accreditation, and can it be documented the same way for Lakemont and Vernon Johns?

Question three

Walnut Hill posts the strongest elementary results in the division and its lowest absenteeism, and carries the lowest displayed performance level. Is the division's documentation keeping pace with its results?
