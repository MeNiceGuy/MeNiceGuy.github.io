Doctoral research on what drives change in home health

Leadership and Learning Predictors of Innovation Adoption in Home Health Agencies

Doctoral capstone, Capella University DBA in Strategy and Innovation, 2026. A quantitative study of 68 managers of privately owned U.S. home health agencies, using validated survey instruments (Boswell, 2026).

The study tested whether two organizational factors, leadership innovation orientation and learning culture, predict how readily home health agencies adopt innovation. The findings are stated exactly as reported, and the study is correlational: it identifies predictors, not causes.
