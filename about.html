Doctoral Research

My capstone, Leadership and Learning Predictors of Innovation Adoption in Home Health Agencies, surveyed 68 managers of privately owned United States home health agencies using validated instruments, including the Dimensions of the Learning Organization Questionnaire and the NoMAD measure. Learning culture emerged as the strongest predictor of innovation adoption, and the two predictors together accounted for 68.1 percent of the variance in the model. The study is correlational; it identifies predictors, not causes, and I report it that way.

Selected Work

Training repayment agreement voided, Virginia, 2026. I analyzed a healthcare employer's training repayment agreement against Virginia wage law and the Fair Labor Standards Act and prepared the written demand. The employer paid all wages owed with no deductions and voided the agreement as contrary to Virginia workforce law.

Section 504 accountability, Virginia, 2024 to 2026. I documented a school division's failure to implement a student's Section 504 plan, advocated for corrective action in writing, and filed a federal complaint with the United States Department of Education, Office for Civil Rights. The division acknowledged gaps in its implementation process, and the matter settled to the client's satisfaction. Client names are held in confidence.

Veteran Directed Care budget audit, Virginia, 2026. Acting as advocate for a program participant, I audited the participant portal against the agency's payroll and scheduling records and diagnosed budget mismanagement: caregiver pay that did not match submitted timesheets, an unexplained negative budget balance, and administrative records that contradicted the approved schedule. I escalated in writing through the supervisory chain to the agency's president and chief executive officer. The agency ordered a leadership review, and a quality assurance specialist took over the case, investigated the findings, corrected the case, and restored it to good standing. Names are held in confidence.

Advisory guidance for agency owners, fourteen states, 2026. I publish written regulatory and operations guidance for home care and residential care owners in national owner communities and professional publications, each answer checked against the governing rule before it posts. Recent work includes Georgia CCSP Medicaid enrollment and its 12-month in-business requirement, CMS GUIDE model respite billing, Florida payer routes for homemaker and companion services, North Carolina PCS referral and assessment rules, Pennsylvania medication administration rules, and referral network fee mechanics.

Background

I started this practice in 2015, as an unincorporated consulting business I ran on the side. It began in music consulting: artist development, music administration, copyright registration, and audience and performance analytics for independent artists. Running creative businesses with the paperwork and the numbers handled right is the foundation this practice still stands on; the music administration continues today through the Boot Mob catalog. Boswell Consulting Agency LLC was formed in 2025, when the work grew into the research-driven health and human services practice it is today. My own path runs from the warehouse floor, through work at Perdue Farms and Amazon, to program operations and doctoral research. I decided on the doctorate while working as a caregiver in the Veteran Directed Care program, after watching avoidable agency problems trace back to weak leadership and training. The through-line is operations: how organizations actually get work done, and where they quietly lose ground.
