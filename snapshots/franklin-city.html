Division Data Snapshot, Fall 2026

Franklin City Public Schools

Franklin City Public Schools serves 1,061 students in two schools in Franklin, Virginia. In 2025-26, 40 percent of its students passed the state reading test and 30 percent passed math, against Virginia rates of 74 and 73 percent, while the Class of 2026 on-time graduation rate of 95 percent ran above the state rate of 93.5 percent. Enrollment. Fall membership was 1,061 students in 2025-26, down from 1,125 in 2024-25 and 1,015 in 2023-24. The total includes 60 pre-kindergarten students, served at S.P. Morton Elementary School. Virginia fall membership in 2025-26 was 1,246,544.

Chronic absenteeism. In 2025-26, 10.9 percent of Franklin City students were absent 10 percent or more of the school year (855 students below 10 percent, 105 at 10 percent or above, in the published counts), below the Virginia rate of 12.9 percent. The division rate fell sharply from 23.3 percent in 2024-25 and 29.6 percent in 2023-24, on the same published counts.

Graduation. The Class of 2026 four-year cohort had 93 students: 88 graduates, an on-time graduation rate of 95 percent, 89 completers, and 1 dropout, a 1 percent dropout rate. Virginia's on-time rate was 93.5 percent with a 4.7 percent dropout rate. Franklin High is the division's only school with grades 9-12, so the school and division cohort figures are the same.

State Oversight

A required state review and agreement, still pending

Franklin City is not under a Memorandum of Understanding with the Virginia Board of Education today. In April 2026, the Board recorded that Franklin City meets the state's criteria for a division-level academic and resource allocation review (DLA-RAR) and a Memorandum of Understanding, based on 2024-25 School Performance and Support Framework results: 50 percent of its schools, one of two, were identified for comprehensive support and improvement. The Board item states that Franklin City will be required to undergo the review and enter a Memorandum of Understanding with the Board.

The Superintendent of Public Instruction recommended the Board take no action on Franklin City at that April 2026 review, with next steps promised for the June 2026 Board meeting. The published June 2026 outcome located covers only the release of three other divisions from their agreements. I located no executed Franklin City agreement and no scheduled review in VDOE's published record as of November 17, 2026, so I report the status as pending, not entered.

The fall 2026 accreditation release does not change that position on its own. The criteria run on framework support identifications, not accreditation alone, and in the fall 2026 release S.P. Morton Elementary remains identified for comprehensive support and improvement while Franklin High is identified for targeted support and improvement. Franklin City has been here before: the Board approved a Memorandum of Understanding for the division in March 2014, and that agreement ended when all of its schools were accredited for 2018-2019. Any new agreement would be a fresh one under the current criteria.

Sources and Method

Where every figure came from

Sources: Virginia Department of Education, School Quality Profiles, Franklin City division profile and Virginia state profile (division SOL pass rates, fall membership, and chronic absenteeism for 2025-26, with 2024-25 and 2023-24 where shown); each school's own School Quality Profile (school fall membership, SOL pass rates, absenteeism, and graduation, read November 17, 2026); accreditation designations and School Performance and Support Framework levels and scores from the fall 2026 release for 2026-27, including the VDOE SPSF School Summary Report 2026 for each school; graduation from the Class of 2026 four-year cohort tables; per-pupil spending from the VDOE Superintendent's Annual Report, Table 15, fiscal year 2025 final; oversight history from Virginia Board of Education agenda item L, April 23, 2026, its Appendix A summary for Franklin City, and Board minutes of June 25, 2026, March 26, 2015, and October 18, 2018. Retrieved November 17, 2026.

Method: I use published all-students figures only. School Quality Profiles suppresses small counts rather than publishing them, shown as < or -, and I never estimate a suppressed value. Virginia accredits schools, not divisions, so this page gives school designations and never a single division rating.

Named gaps: (1) No published outcome specific to Franklin City's promised June 2026 next steps was located; the oversight section rests on the April 2026 Board item and the absence of any announced agreement. (2) School absenteeism is shown as the percentages published on each school's profile; the SPSF reports publish the school rates without student counts, so no school counts are shown.
