One piece of the build, shown finished

A scope is a promise. This is the product. Below is the wage payment policy from Module 2, complete as it would arrive, written for the exact ground my May 2026 letter came from. It protects the employee's pay and the owners' position at the same time, because in the practice's own experience both failures lived in the same missing document.

Policy: Payment of Wages at Separation

1. When employment ends, for any reason, the practice pays all wages due for work performed, in full, on or before the date the employee would have been paid for that work had the employment not ended.

2. No amount is withheld from any paycheck, including a final paycheck, except payroll and withholding taxes or amounts required by law, unless the employee has first given written and signed authorization for that specific deduction.

3. No agreement with the practice requires an employee to forfeit wages for time worked. Any amount the practice believes it is owed, including training costs, is handled as a separate written matter and is never netted against wages.

4. On each pay date the practice provides a written statement showing the hours worked, the rate of pay, the gross wages earned, and the amount and purpose of every deduction, so the employee can determine how the gross and net pay were calculated.

Its verification file

Every policy in the build arrives with this file beside it.

  • Va. Code Section 40.1-29(B): regular pay periods; upon termination, all wages due are paid on or before the date the employee would have been paid had the employment not been terminated.
  • Va. Code Section 40.1-29(D): no employer shall withhold any part of an employee's wages except for taxes or in accordance with law, without the written and signed authorization of the employee; a written statement of earnings is required on each regular pay date.
  • Va. Code Section 40.1-29: no contract or agreement may provide for the forfeiture of an employee's wages for time worked as a condition of employment.

Source: Code of Virginia, Title 40.1, Section 40.1-29, law.lis.virginia.gov. Checked November 14, 2026.
