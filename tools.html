Which tool, when

Use the CMS self-audit when you want to check your agency against CMS expectations before a surveyor does, and see what needs fixing first.

Use the startup cost calculator when you are setting your starting budget and want to work out what starting the agency actually costs before you commit to a number.

Use the EVV number checker when a visit number needs checking before it turns into a billing problem.

Use the bill rate calculator when you are setting or testing an hourly rate, for a private pay client or a staffing contract, and want the rate built from your real tax burden in your state instead of a guessed percentage.
