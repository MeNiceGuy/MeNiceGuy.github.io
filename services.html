How Engagements Run

Written, asynchronous, and verified

"All engagements are delivered in writing on a fully asynchronous model: the client sends materials and data, the firm returns written findings with implementation guides, keeping scope and the record exact." From the firm's capability statement.

You work with the principal

I am a solo consultant. There is no account team and no handoff. The person who diagnoses the problem is the person who writes the findings.

Every claim is traced

Each engagement ships with a verification file that ties every recommendation to its source regulation or policy, so you can cross-check the work yourself.

Billing services

Non-medical Medicaid and waiver billing for home care agencies, run asynchronously through the payer portals and email. No percentage of collections and no phone tag with payers: the work is a defined setup, a cleanup, or a monthly run, each for a flat fee agreed before it starts. The roster is capped at five agencies so the work stays careful.

  • Billing setup and fix: enrollment, EVV, and portal configuration checked and corrected before claims go out
  • Denial cleanup: denied and stuck claims worked through the portal until each one pays or the reason it cannot is documented
  • Monthly billing: claims submitted, remittances reviewed, and denials followed up every cycle

This lane covers non-medical Medicaid and waiver programs. It does not cover Medicare Part A billing, and it never touches patient-balance collections. North Carolina, through NCTracks, is the first state open; other states follow as agencies sign on.
