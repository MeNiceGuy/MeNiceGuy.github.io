How to Start a Home Health Agency in Louisiana

By Dr. Devon Boswell, DBA · November 15, 2026

Louisiana licenses home health agencies as a Home Health Agency license, through the Louisiana Department of Health (LDH), Health Standards Section (HSS). The governing law is R.S. 40:2116.31 (definition cited on LDH Home Health page); LAC 48:I Chapter 91, section 9105 State Licensure (LDH Title 48 text). That state license is the first gate. Medicare certification is the second, and it runs on federal rules that are the same in every state: the Conditions of Participation at 42 CFR Part 484, the Form CMS-855A enrollment, and an initial survey of an agency that is already operating. This page gives you the Louisiana license in its own terms, then the federal layer in the order it actually runs.

One status every new owner has to plan around: CMS has a nationwide moratorium on new home health agency Medicare enrollment, imposed May 13, 2026 and extended on November 12, 2026 to about mid-May 2027. The Louisiana license work below can proceed now. The federal enrollment cannot finish while the moratorium runs, so sequence your spending accordingly.

How to start a home health agency in Louisiana, step by step

  1. Submit a completed home health application form to LDH HSS - LAC 48:I.9105 initial licensure list
  2. Pay the required licensure fee, which is non-refundable, by corporate check, certified check or money order or as LDH determines - LAC 48:I.9105
  3. Submit documentation of a line of credit from a licensed lending agency for at least $75,000 as proof of adequate finances to sustain an agency for at least six months - LAC 48:I.9105
  4. Submit proof of general and professional liability insurance and workers compensation insurance, with coverage of at least $300,000 as stated in the LDH rule text and LDH Home Health page document list
  5. Complete all requirements of the application process within 90 days of the date of initial submission - LAC 48:I.9105

Louisiana home health license fees and costs

  • Initial license fee $600 and renewal fee $600 - HSS Fee Schedule, Home Health row
  • Branch fee $300 per branch - HSS Fee Schedule and LDH Home Health page, Add a Branch
  • Name change fee $25 to print a new license - LDH Home Health page

How long does it take in Louisiana?

  • All requirements of the application process shall be completed within 90 days of initial submission of the home health license application - LAC 48:I.9105

What catches Louisiana applicants

  • LDH is the only licensing authority for home health agencies in Louisiana - LAC 48:I.9105
  • Definition used by LDH: a home health agency provides skilled home health care under a physician order in the place of residence, including skilled nursing and at least one of physical therapy, speech therapy, occupational therapy, medical social services, or home health aide services - LDH Home Health page citing R.S. 40:2116.31
  • Certificate of need: Louisiana uses Facility Need Review (FNR), not a document called a certificate of need. The FNR rule at LAC 48:I Chapter 125 lists the facility and provider types subject to FNR and states designated providers shall first receive FNR approval before applying to be licensed by LDH and before being certified to participate in Title XIX. The LDH Home Health page reviewed did not list an FNR step for a home health agency, so an FNR requirement for home health specifically was not verified from the pages fetched.
  • Medicare order: the pages fetched did not state in one sentence that a state license must be issued before Medicare certification for a new home health agency. LDH Home Health page materials do include CMS forms 855A, CMS-1572 and CMS-1561 in change of ownership documents and state HSS cannot complete that process until receipt of the 855A form from the fiscal intermediary. Verification of the initial Medicare sequence is thin.

The Medicare layer, after the Louisiana license

With the license in hand and the agency operating, the federal sequence is: build to at least 10 skilled patients served with 7 receiving care, file Form CMS-855A through PECOS and pay the $750 institutional enrollment fee (2026), then pass the initial certification survey, through the state survey agency or an accrediting organization with deeming authority (ACHC, CHAP, or The Joint Commission). The full federal walk-through is on Medicare Certification for Home Health Agencies, and the national path is on How to Start a Home Health Agency.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to start a home health agency in Louisiana?

The figures Louisiana publishes are these: Initial license fee $600 and renewal fee $600 - HSS Fee Schedule, Home Health row Branch fee $300 per branch - HSS Fee Schedule and LDH Home Health page, Add a Branch Name change fee $25 to print a new license - LDH Home Health page Beyond the published figures, the operating costs (clinical payroll before certification, insurance, and the reserve operating funds required by 42 CFR 489.28) are larger than every fee on the list.

How long does it take to get a home health license in Louisiana?

All requirements of the application process shall be completed within 90 days of initial submission of the home health license application - LAC 48:I.9105 The federal certification that follows runs on the survey calendar, and new Medicare enrollment is under a CMS moratorium to about mid-May 2027.

What do I need to start a home health agency in Louisiana?

The Home Health Agency license (Louisiana Department of Health (LDH), Health Standards Section (HSS)), an administrator and clinical leadership who meet Louisiana rules, policies written to the Conditions of Participation at 42 CFR Part 484, skilled nursing plus at least one other therapeutic service, OASIS transmission capability, and, before the federal survey, 10 skilled patients served with 7 receiving care. Medicaid enrollment is a separate application after licensure.

How do I get a home health license in Louisiana?

Apply to the Louisiana Department of Health (LDH), Health Standards Section (HSS) under R.S. 40:2116.31 (definition cited on LDH Home Health page); LAC 48:I Chapter 91, section 9105 State Licensure (LDH Title 48 text). The steps on this page follow the sequence the authority publishes. Confirm the current application and fee on the authority site before you file, because fees and forms are updated on the state calendar.
