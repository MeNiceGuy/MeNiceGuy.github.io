How to Start a Group Home in Nevada

By Dr. Devon Boswell, DBA · November 15, 2026

There is no single group home license in Nevada. There is a license for each population the state serves, and the population you choose decides the authority, the rule chapter, and the funding stream. This page lists every residential type Nevada licenses, who runs each one, and the order the application actually runs in.

The group home types Nevada licenses

Residential Facility for Groups

Licensed by: Nevada Health Authority, HCQC. Serves: An aged, infirm, mentally retarded or handicapped person, per the NRS 449.017 definition in legislative text; the term includes, without limitation, an assisted living facility. Establishment that furnishes food, shelter, assistance and limited supervision. Licensed under NRS Chapter 449 and NAC Chapter 449. Fee schedule lists $2,386 initial facility fee plus $200 per bed and $1,193 renewal plus $100 per bed. The Frail Elderly waiver Augmented Personal Care service is provided in licensed Residential Facilities for Groups or an assisted living facility as a 24 hour service, per Nevada Medicaid Provider Type 57 billing guide, and ADSD operates that waiver with Nevada Medicaid.

Home for Individual Residential Care

Licensed by: Nevada Health Authority, HCQC. Serves: Not more than two persons with intellectual disabilities or with physical disabilities or who are aged or infirm, unless related within the third degree of consanguinity or affinity to the provider, per NRS 449.0105. A home in which a natural person furnishes food, shelter, assistance and limited supervision for compensation. HCQC fact sheet describes it as limited to 2 beds, located in licensed private residences, with caregivers present 24/7. Fee schedule lists $1,764 initial facility fee plus $184 per bed.

Supported Living Arrangement services

Licensed by: Aging and Disability Services Division (ADSD), Developmental Services, certification not HCQC licensure. Serves: Persons with intellectual and developmental disabilities. SLA services are certified by ADSD pursuant to NRS 435.3305 to 435.339, per an ADSD exhibit to the Legislature, which states DPBH has no licensing authority over SLA services and SLA services are funded through a Medicaid Home and Community Based Services waiver. ADSD Shared Living Arrangement policy states a maximum of two individuals with an intellectual or developmental disability may reside in one Shared Living Arrangement.

Provider of community based living arrangement services

Licensed by: Nevada Health Authority, HCQC, licensed as a facility for the dependent under NRS Chapter 449. Serves: Persons with mental illness who are served by the Division or any other entity, per NRS 449.0026. Flexible, individualized services, including training and habilitation services, provided in the home for compensation and designed to assist such persons in maximizing independence. NRS 449.0028 states the term does not include rehabilitative residential mental health care.

Facility for intermediate care

Licensed by: Nevada Health Authority, HCQC. Serves: Persons requiring intermediate care, as a facility type listed in the HCQC fee schedule and background instructions. Listed as a distinct licensed facility type in the DPBH fee schedule and in the fingerprint background list for owners or designees. Fee schedule lists $946 initial plus $72 per bed and $473 renewal plus $46 per bed.

How to start a group home in Nevada, step by step

  1. Review NAC Chapter 449 for the facility type
  2. Apply for a new license through HCQC in the CLICS online licensing system and submit all documents on the facility type checklist
  3. Complete fingerprint background investigation for owners or designees of a residential facility for groups and homes for individual residential care, per HCQC background instructions

Nevada group home license fees and costs

  • Residential Facility for Groups: $2,386 initial plus $200 per bed, $1,193 renewal plus $100 per bed, in the DPBH fee schedule under NAC 449.016
  • Home for Individual Residential Care: $1,764 initial plus $184 per bed in the same fee schedule
  • Facility for intermediate care: $946 initial plus $72 per bed, $473 renewal plus $46 per bed in the same fee schedule

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to open a group home in Nevada?

The figures Nevada publishes are these: Residential Facility for Groups: $2,386 initial plus $200 per bed, $1,193 renewal plus $100 per bed, in the DPBH fee schedule under NAC 449.016 Home for Individual Residential Care: $1,764 initial plus $184 per bed in the same fee schedule Facility for intermediate care: $946 initial plus $72 per bed, $473 renewal plus $46 per bed in the same fee schedule The license fee is rarely the largest cost. The home itself, life safety and accessibility work, insurance, and staffing hired before the first placement carry the project.

What are the requirements to open a group home in Nevada?

The license for your population from the authority named on this page, a home that passes health and life safety inspection with local zoning cleared, a qualified administrator or manager, background checks on owners and every staff member, written policies matched to the rule chapter, and staffing that matches the residents' assessed needs. Funded placements require separate Medicaid waiver provider enrollment after the license.

How do I get licensed for a group home in Nevada?

Apply to the authority that licenses homes for your chosen population, listed with each home type on this page. The application, background checks, home inspection, and life safety survey all run through that authority, in the order the steps above follow.
