The Secret Weapon Is Not a Secret. It Is a System.

By Dr. Devon Boswell, DBA · October 23, 2026

Why I wrote Start Your Home Care Agency, and why owners who skip the math rarely make it to year two.

Most home care agencies do not die in the licensing office. They die around month four, when the money runs thin and the owner finally does the math that should have been done before the first dollar went out.

I wrote Start Your Home Care Agency: The Operator's System because that pattern is fixable. The book breaks down the three things that decide whether a new agency survives, in the order they have to be handled.

First, the honest math. What an hour of care actually costs you once caregiver pay, taxes, insurance, and overhead are in. The rate you need to charge to break even. Which payers work in your market and which ones quietly lose you money. That is Chapter 1, before licensing, on purpose. Owners who run these numbers first make every later decision with their eyes open.

Second, the order. Entity, EIN, insurance, license, policies, enrollment, hiring, first clients. The work has a sequence, and doing it out of order is how owners lose months, pay fees twice, and sit idle waiting on a step they could have finished weeks earlier. The book walks the sequence step by step, in any state.

Third, the receipts. Every regulatory claim in the book is sourced, so you do not have to take my word for anything. You can check each requirement yourself before you act on it. And in Chapter 12 I bring in my doctoral research, a study of 68 managers of privately owned home health agencies, on why some agencies adapt when things change and others stall. The agencies that build learning into daily operations are the ones that adopt what works.

People ask what separates the owners who make it from the ones who close. It is not talent and it is not luck. The owners who make it are running a system while everyone else is guessing from comment threads and outdated blog posts. Knowing your numbers, your sequence, and your requirements before you spend is the whole advantage. That is what this book hands you.

The Kindle edition is on pre-order now, and the paperback and hardcover land November 1. If you are starting an agency, or you are in your first year and the numbers still do not feel solid, this was written for you.
