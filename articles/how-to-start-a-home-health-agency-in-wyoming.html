How to Start a Home Health Agency in Wyoming

By Dr. Devon Boswell, DBA · November 15, 2026

Wyoming licenses home health agencies as a Home Health Agency license, through the Wyoming Department of Health, Aging Division, Healthcare Licensing and Surveys (HLS). The governing law is Wyo. Stat. section 35-2-901 et seq.; Wyoming Department of Health Rules, Home Health Agency Chapter 10 (Licensure) and Chapter 9 (Program Administration). That state license is the first gate. Medicare certification is the second, and it runs on federal rules that are the same in every state: the Conditions of Participation at 42 CFR Part 484, the Form CMS-855A enrollment, and an initial survey of an agency that is already operating. This page gives you the Wyoming license in its own terms, then the federal layer in the order it actually runs.

One status every new owner has to plan around: CMS has a nationwide moratorium on new home health agency Medicare enrollment, imposed May 13, 2026 and extended on November 12, 2026 to about mid-May 2027. The Wyoming license work below can proceed now. The federal enrollment cannot finish while the moratorium runs, so sequence your spending accordingly.

How to start a home health agency in Wyoming, step by step

  1. Fill out the State license application in its entirety and submit it to HLS along with the required fee
  2. Send the required paperwork on the Home Health Agency Licensure Checklist to HLS for review and approval before the agency can receive a State license
  3. Once HLS has approved these items, a provisional license will be issued and the agency may start providing services to clients, patients, and residents
  4. When the agency has clients for whom it is providing two or more services, request in writing that a health survey be conducted; an unannounced survey will be provided by state surveyors as soon as scheduling permits
  5. When the initial survey has been completed and the facility is found to be in compliance with the regulations, a permanent license will be issued
  6. For Medicare participation, complete a CMS-855A or CMS-855B enrollment application and submit it directly to the fiscal intermediary or carrier; the state agency will not perform the federal survey until the intermediary or carrier notifies it of review and recommendations to CMS

What catches Wyoming applicants

  • A valid state license is required prior to providing care to any client, patient, or resident, so state licensure comes before operation and is separate from Medicare certification
  • A state license permits an applicant to operate a healthcare facility in Wyoming; it does not enroll or certify a provider or supplier to participate in federal certification programs (Medicare/Medicaid), per the HLS home health page
  • For Medicare, HLS certifies and periodically recertifies providers to assure Medicare Conditions of Participation are met, and such approval is prerequisite to qualifying to participate in the state Medicaid program as well
  • The HLS page requires the application to be submitted along with the required fee but does not print the fee amount on the page, so no fee is listed here
  • Home Health Agencies are also required to be licensed by the State of Wyoming, restated in the federal certification section of the HLS page
  • No certificate of need step is printed on the HLS home health page reviewed

The Medicare layer, after the Wyoming license

With the license in hand and the agency operating, the federal sequence is: build to at least 10 skilled patients served with 7 receiving care, file Form CMS-855A through PECOS and pay the $750 institutional enrollment fee (2026), then pass the initial certification survey, through the state survey agency or an accrediting organization with deeming authority (ACHC, CHAP, or The Joint Commission). The full federal walk-through is on Medicare Certification for Home Health Agencies, and the national path is on How to Start a Home Health Agency.

Frequently asked questions

What do I need to start a home health agency in Wyoming?

The Home Health Agency license (Wyoming Department of Health, Aging Division, Healthcare Licensing and Surveys (HLS)), an administrator and clinical leadership who meet Wyoming rules, policies written to the Conditions of Participation at 42 CFR Part 484, skilled nursing plus at least one other therapeutic service, OASIS transmission capability, and, before the federal survey, 10 skilled patients served with 7 receiving care. Medicaid enrollment is a separate application after licensure.

How do I get a home health license in Wyoming?

Apply to the Wyoming Department of Health, Aging Division, Healthcare Licensing and Surveys (HLS) under Wyo. Stat. section 35-2-901 et seq.; Wyoming Department of Health Rules, Home Health Agency Chapter 10 (Licensure) and Chapter 9 (Program Administration). The steps on this page follow the sequence the authority publishes. Confirm the current application and fee on the authority site before you file, because fees and forms are updated on the state calendar.
