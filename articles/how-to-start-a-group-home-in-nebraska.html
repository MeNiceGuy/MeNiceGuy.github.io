How to Start a Group Home in Nebraska

By Dr. Devon Boswell, DBA · November 15, 2026

There is no single group home license in Nebraska. There is a license for each population the state serves, and the population you choose decides the authority, the rule chapter, and the funding stream. This page lists every residential type Nebraska licenses, who runs each one, and the order the application actually runs in.

The group home types Nebraska licenses

Assisted Living Facility (ALF)

Licensed by: Nebraska Department of Health and Human Services, Division of Public Health, Licensure Unit. Serves: Four or more residents who need shelter, food, and assistance with care activities continuously for 24 or more hours. Licensed under Title 175 Chapter 4. Anyone who provides assisted living services for four or more residents must be licensed as an ALF. An ALF is not a nursing home and cannot provide routine care by licensed nurses (RN or LPN). Initial and renewal fees by beds: 1 to 10 beds $950, 11 to 20 beds $1,450, 21 to 50 beds $1,650, 51 or more beds $1,950 (175 NAC 4-004.09, printed on the DHHS page). Nebraska Medicaid Aged and Disabled Waiver and Traumatic Brain Injury Waiver list Supported Residential Living, which can only be provided by an agency licensed as an assisted living provider.

Center for Persons with Developmental Disabilities (CDD)

Licensed by: Nebraska Department of Health and Human Services, Division of Public Health. Serves: Four or more persons residing at the facility who have developmental disabilities. DHHS describes this license as a center or group home for persons with developmental disabilities. Shelter, food, and care, advice, counseling, diagnosis, treatment, or related services for more than 24 consecutive hours. Care includes supervision and assistance with personal care, activities of daily living, and health maintenance. Licensed under Title 175 Chapter 3. Initial and renewal fee $150, duplicate $10. Licenses expire November 30 each year. Onsite inspection within 30 working days of a completed application, and inspection and license issuance must occur prior to client use and occupancy. Funded placements are under the Medicaid HCBS Comprehensive Developmental Disabilities Waiver and related DD waivers (Title 403 and Title 404).

Intermediate Care Facility for Persons with Developmental Disabilities / Intermediate Care Facility for Individuals with Intellectual Disabilities (ICF/IID)

Licensed by: Nebraska Department of Health and Human Services, Division of Public Health. Serves: Four or more persons residing at the facility who have developmental disabilities or related conditions, including epilepsy and cerebral palsy. Shelter, food, and training or habilitation services, advice, counseling, diagnosis, treatment, care, nursing care, or related services for more than 24 consecutive hours. Licensed under Title 175 Chapter 17. Certification means compliance with federal requirements at 42 CFR Part 483 Subpart I and eligibility for Medicaid payment, and the facility must have a Nebraska license before it can apply for certification and maintain certification. Licenses expire March 31 each year per the DHHS FAQ page. Initial onsite inspection within 30 working days of a completed application.

Mental Health Substance Use Treatment Center

Licensed by: Nebraska Department of Health and Human Services, Division of Public Health, Licensure Unit. Serves: Individuals living at the facility who have a mental illness, a substance use disorder, or both. Provides shelter, food, counseling, supervision, diagnosis and treatment, and other assistance for individuals living at the facility for more than 24 consecutive hours. A facility may serve mental illness, substance use disorders, or both. Licensed under Title 175 Chapter 18 and the Health Care Facility Licensure Act. Initial and renewal fees: 1 to 16 beds $250, 17 to 50 beds $275, 51 or more beds $300. Licenses expire September 30 each year. The facility cannot operate until completion of an onsite inspection and the Licensure Unit has issued a license; initial inspection is onsite within 30 working days of receiving a completed application.

Residential Child-Caring Agency

Licensed by: Nebraska Department of Health and Human Services, Division of Public Health, Office of Children Services Licensing. Serves: Children, four or more, in out-of-home placement other than a foster family home. Licensed under the Children Residential Facilities and Placing Licensure Act, Neb. Rev. Stat. sections 71-1924 to 71-1951. A residential child-caring agency provides care for four or more children and is not a foster family home as defined in section 71-1901. Care means room and board and responsibility for safety and welfare on a 24-hour per day basis. The DHHS roster notes licensed programs approved for emergency shelter care, group home care, and PRTF or RTC care.

How to start a group home in Nebraska, step by step

  1. Assisted living: submit a written application, submit the required fee, and pass an on-site inspection when required (DHHS Assisted Living Facilities page)
  2. Center for Persons with Developmental Disabilities: contact DHHS Division of Public Health for an initial licensure packet, submit a completed written application with required documentation and fee, and pass an onsite inspection prior to client use and occupancy (DHHS CDD page)
  3. Intermediate Care Facility for Persons with Developmental Disabilities: contact the Licensure Unit for an initial licensure packet, submit a completed application with required documents and appropriate fees, Division reviews for completeness, Division conducts an onsite inspection within 30 working days, facility must be furnished and ready for operation prior to the initial inspection, and the Division decides on issuance after the inspection (DHHS ICF/DD FAQ page)
  4. Mental Health Substance Use Treatment Center: contact the Licensure Unit for an initial license application, submit the completed application, required documentation, and licensing fee, and do not operate until the onsite inspection is complete and a license is issued (DHHS MHSU page)

Nebraska group home license fees and costs

  • Assisted Living Facility initial and renewal: 1 to 10 beds $950, 11 to 20 beds $1,450, 21 to 50 beds $1,650, 51 or more beds $1,950 - 175 NAC 4-004.09 (DHHS page)
  • Center for Persons with Developmental Disabilities initial and renewal: $150, duplicate license $10 - 175 NAC 3-002.04A (DHHS page)
  • Mental Health Substance Use Treatment Center initial and renewal: 1 to 16 beds $250, 17 to 50 beds $275, 51 or more beds $300 (DHHS page)

How long does it take in Nebraska?

  • Assisted living renewal: notice of renewal is sent no later than 30 days prior to the license expiration date (DHHS page)
  • Center for Persons with Developmental Disabilities: licenses expire November 30 each year; onsite inspection within 30 working days of a completed application (DHHS page and FAQ)
  • Intermediate Care Facility for Persons with Developmental Disabilities: licenses expire March 31 each year; onsite inspection within 30 working days of a completed application (DHHS FAQ)
  • Mental Health Substance Use Treatment Center: licenses expire September 30 each year; initial inspection within 30 working days of receiving a completed application (DHHS page)

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to open a group home in Nebraska?

The figures Nebraska publishes are these: Assisted Living Facility initial and renewal: 1 to 10 beds $950, 11 to 20 beds $1,450, 21 to 50 beds $1,650, 51 or more beds $1,950 - 175 NAC 4-004.09 (DHHS page) Center for Persons with Developmental Disabilities initial and renewal: $150, duplicate license $10 - 175 NAC 3-002.04A (DHHS page) Mental Health Substance Use Treatment Center initial and renewal: 1 to 16 beds $250, 17 to 50 beds $275, 51 or more beds $300 (DHHS page) The license fee is rarely the largest cost. The home itself, life safety and accessibility work, insurance, and staffing hired before the first placement carry the project.

How long does it take to get a group home license in Nebraska?

Assisted living renewal: notice of renewal is sent no later than 30 days prior to the license expiration date (DHHS page) Center for Persons with Developmental Disabilities: licenses expire November 30 each year; onsite inspection within 30 working days of a completed application (DHHS page and FAQ) Intermediate Care Facility for Persons with Developmental Disabilities: licenses expire March 31 each year; onsite inspection within 30 working days of a completed application (DHHS FAQ) Mental Health Substance Use Treatment Center: licenses expire September 30 each year; initial inspection within 30 working days of receiving a completed application (DHHS page)

What are the requirements to open a group home in Nebraska?

The license for your population from the authority named on this page, a home that passes health and life safety inspection with local zoning cleared, a qualified administrator or manager, background checks on owners and every staff member, written policies matched to the rule chapter, and staffing that matches the residents' assessed needs. Funded placements require separate Medicaid waiver provider enrollment after the license.

How do I get licensed for a group home in Nebraska?

Apply to the authority that licenses homes for your chosen population, listed with each home type on this page. The application, background checks, home inspection, and life safety survey all run through that authority, in the order the steps above follow.
