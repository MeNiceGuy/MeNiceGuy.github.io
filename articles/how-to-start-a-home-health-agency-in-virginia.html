How to Start a Home Health Agency in Virginia

By Dr. Devon Boswell, DBA · November 15, 2026

Virginia licenses home health agencies as a Home Care Organization license with skilled services (HCO skilled), with a Medicare-certified layer on the skilled HCO license, through the Virginia Department of Health, Office of Licensure and Certification (VDH OLC). The governing law is 12VAC5-381, Regulations for the Licensure of Home Care Organizations, skilled services at 12VAC5-381-300 to -360. That state license is the first gate. Medicare certification is the second, and it runs on federal rules that are the same in every state: the Conditions of Participation at 42 CFR Part 484, the Form CMS-855A enrollment, and an initial survey of an agency that is already operating. This page gives you the Virginia license in its own terms, then the federal layer in the order it actually runs.

One status every new owner has to plan around: CMS has a nationwide moratorium on new home health agency Medicare enrollment, imposed May 13, 2026 and extended on November 12, 2026 to about mid-May 2027. The Virginia license work below can proceed now. The federal enrollment cannot finish while the moratorium runs, so sequence your spending accordingly.

How to start a home health agency in Virginia, step by step

  1. Form the business entity and register with the Virginia State Corporation Commission (SCC)
  2. Get an EIN
  3. Prepare policies and procedures for the areas listed at 12VAC5-381-180
  4. Identify the administrator and clinical leadership (administrator requirements at 12VAC5-381-170)
  5. Prepare financials showing a budget and proof of funds covering the first three months of operation (12VAC5-381-190, the three-month reserve)
  6. Apply for the Home Care Organization license with skilled services to VDH OLC under 12VAC5-381
  7. Prepare for inspection or survey and be ready to file a plan of correction (12VAC5-381-80 and -100)
  8. Set up HIPAA-compliant records, staff onboarding, and criminal background checks (12VAC5-381-110 and Code of Virginia section 32.1-162.9:1)
  9. For Medicare certification, enroll on the CMS-855A through PECOS through CGS Administrators and meet 42 CFR Part 484
  10. Enroll separately with DMAS through the MES Provider Resources site for Medicaid payment

Virginia home health license fees and costs

  • HCO license: $1,500 initial application fee and $1,500 renewal fee, effective May 22, 2025 (12VAC5-381-70)
  • Exemption request by a licensed organization: $75 one-time processing fee (12VAC5-381-70(D))
  • Hospice license, for comparison in the same VDH system: $500 initial and $500 renewal, annual license (12VAC5-391-70)

How long does it take in Virginia?

  • VDH OLC published estimate: about 10 to 12 months from submission to approval for a complete initial HCO application, with applications processed in order received (checked 2026-11-11)
  • HCO license term is triennial (three years); the hospice license in the same system is annual

What catches Virginia applicants

  • Virginia has no separate non-medical home care license; personal care services sit inside the HCO definition in 12VAC5-381, and there is one HCO license covering non-medical and skilled tracks
  • Exemption is narrow: the chapter does not apply to providers of only homemaker, chore, or companion services (12VAC5-381-30A); hands-on personal care ends the exemption
  • No certificate of need is stated in the research for an HCO home health license; a Certificate of Public Need is required before nursing facility licensure (12VAC5-371-40.E), which is a different track
  • Medicaid enrollment is separate from licensure: all new providers have been screened and enrolled with DMAS through MES since April 4, 2022, and agency-directed personal care runs through the CCC Plus waiver layer at 12VAC30-120-930
  • Medicare certification is a layer on the skilled HCO license, not a substitute for the state license
  • Unlicensed operation of a home care organization is a Class 6 felony (Code of Virginia section 32.1-162.15)

The Medicare layer, after the Virginia license

With the license in hand and the agency operating, the federal sequence is: build to at least 10 skilled patients served with 7 receiving care, file Form CMS-855A through PECOS and pay the $750 institutional enrollment fee (2026), then pass the initial certification survey, through the state survey agency or an accrediting organization with deeming authority (ACHC, CHAP, or The Joint Commission). The full federal walk-through is on Medicare Certification for Home Health Agencies, and the national path is on How to Start a Home Health Agency.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to start a home health agency in Virginia?

The figures Virginia publishes are these: HCO license: $1,500 initial application fee and $1,500 renewal fee, effective May 22, 2025 (12VAC5-381-70) Exemption request by a licensed organization: $75 one-time processing fee (12VAC5-381-70(D)) Hospice license, for comparison in the same VDH system: $500 initial and $500 renewal, annual license (12VAC5-391-70) Beyond the published figures, the operating costs (clinical payroll before certification, insurance, and the reserve operating funds required by 42 CFR 489.28) are larger than every fee on the list.

How long does it take to get a home health license in Virginia?

VDH OLC published estimate: about 10 to 12 months from submission to approval for a complete initial HCO application, with applications processed in order received (checked 2026-11-11) HCO license term is triennial (three years); the hospice license in the same system is annual The federal certification that follows runs on the survey calendar, and new Medicare enrollment is under a CMS moratorium to about mid-May 2027.

What do I need to start a home health agency in Virginia?

The Home Care Organization license with skilled services (HCO skilled), with a Medicare-certified layer on the skilled HCO license (Virginia Department of Health, Office of Licensure and Certification (VDH OLC)), an administrator and clinical leadership who meet Virginia rules, policies written to the Conditions of Participation at 42 CFR Part 484, skilled nursing plus at least one other therapeutic service, OASIS transmission capability, and, before the federal survey, 10 skilled patients served with 7 receiving care. Medicaid enrollment is a separate application after licensure.

How do I get a home health license in Virginia?

Apply to the Virginia Department of Health, Office of Licensure and Certification (VDH OLC) under 12VAC5-381, Regulations for the Licensure of Home Care Organizations, skilled services at 12VAC5-381-300 to -360. The steps on this page follow the sequence the authority publishes. Confirm the current application and fee on the authority site before you file, because fees and forms are updated on the state calendar.
