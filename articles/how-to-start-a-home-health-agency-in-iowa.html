How to Start a Home Health Agency in Iowa

By Dr. Devon Boswell, DBA · November 15, 2026

Iowa does not issue a separate state home health agency license. Home health agencies in Iowa operate as Medicare certified agencies under the federal definition at 42 CFR Part 484, and the Iowa Department of Inspections, Appeals, and Licensing (DIAL), Health Facilities Division, acts as the state survey and certification agency. Iowa Code Chapter 135J covers hospice, not home health. The gate is the federal certification sequence, run with DIAL as the survey agency, and this page lays it out in order.

One status every new owner has to plan around: CMS has a nationwide moratorium on new home health agency Medicare enrollment, imposed May 13, 2026 and extended on November 12, 2026 to about mid-May 2027. The state-side entry work below can proceed now. The federal enrollment cannot finish while the moratorium runs, so sequence your spending accordingly.

How to start a home health agency in Iowa, step by step

  1. Confirm the agency meets the federal home health agency definition used by DIAL: skilled nursing services for patients in their homes plus at least one other therapeutic service
  2. Pursue federal Medicare certification, with DIAL Health Facilities Division acting as the state survey agency that determines compliance with federal certification requirements
  3. Enroll separately with Iowa Medicaid through Iowa HHS if the agency will serve Medicaid members, including HCBS waiver members

What catches Iowa applicants

  • Iowa does not license home health agencies at the state level. DIAL lists home health agencies among the entities it surveys and certifies, and describes them by the federal definition in Title 42 CFR, with no Iowa home health agency license type on its Health Facilities list.
  • Because there is no state license, there is no state license fee and no state license to obtain before Medicare certification; the state role is the federal survey and certification function.
  • No certificate of need requirement for a home health agency was printed on the DIAL or Iowa Code sources fetched for this sheet; Iowa certificate of need under Iowa Code sections 10A.711 to 10A.723 and 135.61 to 135.83 applies to institutional health facilities, and an assisted living program certified under Chapter 231C is expressly exempt from section 10A.713 certificate of need requirements in the bill text reviewed. Verification of CON for home health specifically was thin.
  • DIAL also registers health care employment agencies under Iowa Code Chapter 135Q and 481 IAC Chapter 55; that registration covers agencies that supply workers to health care entities and is not a home health agency license.

The Medicare layer

With the state-side entry handled and the agency operating, the federal sequence is: build to at least 10 skilled patients served with 7 receiving care, file Form CMS-855A through PECOS and pay the $750 institutional enrollment fee (2026), then pass the initial certification survey, through the state survey agency or an accrediting organization with deeming authority (ACHC, CHAP, or The Joint Commission). The full federal walk-through is on Medicare Certification for Home Health Agencies, and the national path is on How to Start a Home Health Agency.

Frequently asked questions

What do I need to start a home health agency in Iowa?

Medicare certification as a home health agency, because Iowa has no state home health license. Beyond that: an administrator and clinical leadership, policies written to the Conditions of Participation at 42 CFR Part 484, skilled nursing plus at least one other therapeutic service, OASIS transmission capability, and, before the federal survey, 10 skilled patients served with 7 receiving care. Any state gate named on this page, such as a certificate of need where the state requires one, comes first, and Medicaid enrollment is a separate application.

Do I need a state license to start a home health agency in Iowa?

No. Medicare-certified home health agency (federal certification only, no separate Iowa state home health agency license) The operative gate is Medicare certification, which follows the federal sequence laid out on this page.
