How to Start a Home Health Agency in Idaho

By Dr. Devon Boswell, DBA · November 15, 2026

Idaho no longer issues state home health agency licenses. Under the Streamlined Facilities Licensing and Inspection Act, effective July 1, 2025, Medicare certified home health agencies are not required to be state licensed and no new state licenses are issued. The Bureau of Facility Standards remains the state survey agency for federal certification. The gate is the CMS enrollment packet and the certification survey, and this page lays out that sequence in order.

One status every new owner has to plan around: CMS has a nationwide moratorium on new home health agency Medicare enrollment, imposed May 13, 2026 and extended on November 12, 2026 to about mid-May 2027. The state-side entry work below can proceed now. The federal enrollment cannot finish while the moratorium runs, so sequence your spending accordingly.

How to start a home health agency in Idaho, step by step

  1. Review the CMS Enrollment and Certification Roadmap before using the packet information, per the BFS Home Health Agencies page
  2. Request, complete and submit an application packet to establish CMS Medicare certification of a Home Health Agency; materials are on the BFS page or from BFS at 208-334-6626
  3. Email the items that must be reviewed and approved prior to an initial survey: CMS 1561 Health Insurance Benefits Agreement, Fiscal Year Ending form, and CMS 1572a Home Health Agency Survey and Deficiencies Report, completing sections 1 to 22
  4. Complete CMS-855A, Medicare Application for Health Care Providers that will Bill Fiscal Intermediaries, and forward it to the Regional Home Health Intermediary, or use PECOS in lieu of the paper CMS-855A
  5. Complete Office of Civil Rights clearance for Medicare certification online through the Assurance of Compliance Portal and submit a copy of the OCR email to the State Agency for the CMS Regional Office
  6. To become an Idaho Medicaid provider, submit a separate Idaho Medicaid Provider Enrollment application to Gainwell through idmedicaid.com; the BFS page states Medicaid enrollment is a separate process from federal certification and state licensure

How long does it take in Idaho?

  • Medicare and Medicaid reimbursement is not retroactive and usually becomes effective only after the enrollment application is approved, the initial survey is completed, and the agency is in compliance with all regulations or has submitted an acceptable plan of correction, per the BFS Home Health Agencies page

What catches Idaho applicants

  • Plainly: in accordance with the Streamlined Facilities Licensing and Inspection Act, effective July 1, 2025, Medicare Certified Home Health Agencies are no longer required to be licensed in the State of Idaho, and no new licenses will be issued after July 1, 2025, per the BFS Home Health Agencies page
  • A Home Health Agency in the BFS definition provides Skilled Nursing Services and at least one other therapeutic service in the place of residence used as the patient home: Physical Therapy, Speech Language Pathology, Occupational Therapy, Medical Social Services, or Home Health Aide Services
  • No state application fee for home health is printed on the BFS page, so no fee is listed
  • No certificate of need for home health was identified in the official Idaho sources reviewed

The Medicare layer

With the state-side entry handled and the agency operating, the federal sequence is: build to at least 10 skilled patients served with 7 receiving care, file Form CMS-855A through PECOS and pay the $750 institutional enrollment fee (2026), then pass the initial certification survey, through the state survey agency or an accrediting organization with deeming authority (ACHC, CHAP, or The Joint Commission). The full federal walk-through is on Medicare Certification for Home Health Agencies, and the national path is on How to Start a Home Health Agency.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to start a home health agency in Idaho?

Medicare and Medicaid reimbursement is not retroactive and usually becomes effective only after the enrollment application is approved, the initial survey is completed, and the agency is in compliance with all regulations or has submitted an acceptable plan of correction, per the BFS Home Health Agencies page The federal certification that follows runs on the survey calendar, and new Medicare enrollment is under a CMS moratorium to about mid-May 2027.

What do I need to start a home health agency in Idaho?

Medicare certification as a home health agency, because Idaho has no state home health license. Beyond that: an administrator and clinical leadership, policies written to the Conditions of Participation at 42 CFR Part 484, skilled nursing plus at least one other therapeutic service, OASIS transmission capability, and, before the federal survey, 10 skilled patients served with 7 receiving care. Any state gate named on this page, such as a certificate of need where the state requires one, comes first, and Medicaid enrollment is a separate application.

Do I need a state license to start a home health agency in Idaho?

No. No state home health agency license issued after July 1, 2025 - CMS Medicare certification of a Home Health Agency only The operative gate is Medicare certification, which follows the federal sequence laid out on this page.
