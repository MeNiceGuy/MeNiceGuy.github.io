How to Start a Home Health Agency in Delaware

By Dr. Devon Boswell, DBA · November 15, 2026

Delaware licenses home health agencies as a Skilled Home Health Agency (HHAS) license. A separate Home Health Agency - Aide Only (HHAAO) license covers aide-only agencies, through the Delaware Department of Health and Social Services (DHSS), Division of Health Care Quality (DHCQ), Office of Health Facilities Licensing and Certification (OHFLC). The governing law is 16 Del. C. sec. 122(3)o and 16 DE Admin. Code 3350, Skilled Home Health Agencies (Licensure). Aide Only is 16 DE Admin. Code 3351. That state license is the first gate. Medicare certification is the second, and it runs on federal rules that are the same in every state: the Conditions of Participation at 42 CFR Part 484, the Form CMS-855A enrollment, and an initial survey of an agency that is already operating. This page gives you the Delaware license in its own terms, then the federal layer in the order it actually runs.

One status every new owner has to plan around: CMS has a nationwide moratorium on new home health agency Medicare enrollment, imposed May 13, 2026 and extended on November 12, 2026 to about mid-May 2027. The Delaware license work below can proceed now. The federal enrollment cannot finish while the moratorium runs, so sequence your spending accordingly.

How to start a home health agency in Delaware, step by step

  1. Read the regulations for the specific agency type
  2. Complete the application and checklist in its entirety with the required nonrefundable fee, certified check or money order only, prepare all documents in a binder according to checklist instructions, and deliver to OHFLC at 263 Chapman Road, Suite 200, Newark, DE 19702-5408
  3. The Division reviews the application for completeness. An incomplete application is returned to the applicant to pick up with a list of missing items
  4. Application accepted for further review. The applicant receives an email stating it was forwarded for review and that the time frame for review is 6 to 9 months or longer
  5. Final license disposition: if approved, the initial license will be issued for the first 90 days. If revisions are requested, the agency has 30 days to respond
  6. Unannounced survey to confirm compliance. Passing the survey leads to an annual license. Agencies with deficiencies must complete an acceptable plan of correction before getting an annual license

Delaware home health license fees and costs

  • Initial licensure fee $500 for a Skilled Home Health Agency, as printed on the official OHFLC Part 1 Initial Licensure Application for 3350 Home Health Agency Skilled (HHAS)
  • Annual licensure fee $300 for home health agencies, as printed on the Delaware First Steps official license listing for Home Health Agencies

How long does it take in Delaware?

  • Binder review time frame stated by DHCQ: 6 to 9 months or longer
  • Initial license issued for the first 90 days
  • Annual license after the unannounced survey, if in substantial compliance

What catches Delaware applicants

  • A Skilled Home Health Agency must provide two or more home care services, one of which must be licensed nursing services or home health aide services. Services listed by DHCQ include skilled nursing, physical therapy, occupational therapy, and speech and language pathology by licensed professionals.
  • State licensure is the DHCQ process described on the Opening a Home Health Agency page. That page directs applicants not to spend money on business licenses, insurance, office space, or hiring personnel until recommended for licensure by OHFLC.
  • A separate license is required for each office maintained by a home health agency, per Regulation 2.1.2 as quoted on the DHCQ page.
  • The official Opening a Home Health Agency page fetched for this sheet does not list a certificate of need or Certificate of Public Review step.
  • The DHCQ FAQ on that page states OHFLC does not regulate group homes and directs group home questions to the Division of Developmental Disabilities Services, which is separate from the DHCQ long-term care facility licenses listed in the group home section below.

The Medicare layer, after the Delaware license

With the license in hand and the agency operating, the federal sequence is: build to at least 10 skilled patients served with 7 receiving care, file Form CMS-855A through PECOS and pay the $750 institutional enrollment fee (2026), then pass the initial certification survey, through the state survey agency or an accrediting organization with deeming authority (ACHC, CHAP, or The Joint Commission). The full federal walk-through is on Medicare Certification for Home Health Agencies, and the national path is on How to Start a Home Health Agency.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to start a home health agency in Delaware?

The figures Delaware publishes are these: Initial licensure fee $500 for a Skilled Home Health Agency, as printed on the official OHFLC Part 1 Initial Licensure Application for 3350 Home Health Agency Skilled (HHAS) Annual licensure fee $300 for home health agencies, as printed on the Delaware First Steps official license listing for Home Health Agencies Beyond the published figures, the operating costs (clinical payroll before certification, insurance, and the reserve operating funds required by 42 CFR 489.28) are larger than every fee on the list.

How long does it take to get a home health license in Delaware?

Binder review time frame stated by DHCQ: 6 to 9 months or longer Initial license issued for the first 90 days Annual license after the unannounced survey, if in substantial compliance The federal certification that follows runs on the survey calendar, and new Medicare enrollment is under a CMS moratorium to about mid-May 2027.

What do I need to start a home health agency in Delaware?

The Skilled Home Health Agency (HHAS) license. A separate Home Health Agency - Aide Only (HHAAO) license covers aide-only agencies (Delaware Department of Health and Social Services (DHSS), Division of Health Care Quality (DHCQ), Office of Health Facilities Licensing and Certification (OHFLC)), an administrator and clinical leadership who meet Delaware rules, policies written to the Conditions of Participation at 42 CFR Part 484, skilled nursing plus at least one other therapeutic service, OASIS transmission capability, and, before the federal survey, 10 skilled patients served with 7 receiving care. Medicaid enrollment is a separate application after licensure.

How do I get a home health license in Delaware?

Apply to the Delaware Department of Health and Social Services (DHSS), Division of Health Care Quality (DHCQ), Office of Health Facilities Licensing and Certification (OHFLC) under 16 Del. C. sec. 122(3)o and 16 DE Admin. Code 3350, Skilled Home Health Agencies (Licensure). Aide Only is 16 DE Admin. Code 3351. The steps on this page follow the sequence the authority publishes. Confirm the current application and fee on the authority site before you file, because fees and forms are updated on the state calendar.
