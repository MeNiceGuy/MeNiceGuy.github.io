How to Start a Home Health Agency in Kentucky

By Dr. Devon Boswell, DBA · November 15, 2026

Kentucky licenses home health agencies as a Home Health Agency license, through the Kentucky Cabinet for Health and Family Services (CHFS), Office of Inspector General (OIG), Division of Health Care. The governing law is 902 KAR 20:081 (home health agency licensure regulation cited in CHFS and Kentucky regulation references); 907 KAR 1:030 (home health agency services for Kentucky Medicaid). That state license is the first gate. Medicare certification is the second, and it runs on federal rules that are the same in every state: the Conditions of Participation at 42 CFR Part 484, the Form CMS-855A enrollment, and an initial survey of an agency that is already operating. This page gives you the Kentucky license in its own terms, then the federal layer in the order it actually runs.

One status every new owner has to plan around: CMS has a nationwide moratorium on new home health agency Medicare enrollment, imposed May 13, 2026 and extended on November 12, 2026 to about mid-May 2027. The Kentucky license work below can proceed now. The federal enrollment cannot finish while the moratorium runs, so sequence your spending accordingly.

How to start a home health agency in Kentucky, step by step

  1. Contact the Office of Inspector General for a Certificate of Need and license - CHFS Medicaid Home Health Services page
  2. Determine whether a certificate of need or fire marshal approval applies before submitting the application; OIG instructs applicants for other health care facilities to contact its office before submitting to determine whether these requirements apply - CHFS OIG applications page
  3. Submit the Home Health Agency Application listed by OIG under Other Health Care Facilities - CHFS OIG applications page
  4. Enroll in Medicare - listed as a qualification to enroll and bill Kentucky Medicaid on the CHFS home health page
  5. Enroll as an active Kentucky Medicaid provider, Provider Type 34, and if applicable enroll with the managed care organization of any beneficiary served - CHFS home health page

Kentucky home health license fees and costs

  • Home Health Agency $500 - OIG License Fee Schedule for Other Health Care Facilities, revised 10/15/2026

What catches Kentucky applicants

  • Certificate of need: required step for home health in Kentucky as presented by CHFS. The CHFS Medicaid home health page states that in Kentucky, home health service providers must contact the Office of Inspector General for a Certificate of Need and license.
  • State license before Medicare and Medicaid: CHFS Medicaid home health page lists the order of qualifications to enroll and bill Kentucky Medicaid as enrolled in Medicare, licensed by the state where it practices, and enrolled as an active Medicaid provider. It does not, in the text fetched, state that the Kentucky license must issue before Medicare enrollment begins.
  • Services under Kentucky Medicaid: physician-prescribed, written plan of care, short-term, including intermittent skilled nursing, physical, speech and occupational therapies, non-routine medical supplies, medical social services and home health aide services - CHFS home health page, citing 907 KAR 1:030.

The Medicare layer, after the Kentucky license

With the license in hand and the agency operating, the federal sequence is: build to at least 10 skilled patients served with 7 receiving care, file Form CMS-855A through PECOS and pay the $750 institutional enrollment fee (2026), then pass the initial certification survey, through the state survey agency or an accrediting organization with deeming authority (ACHC, CHAP, or The Joint Commission). The full federal walk-through is on Medicare Certification for Home Health Agencies, and the national path is on How to Start a Home Health Agency.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to start a home health agency in Kentucky?

The figures Kentucky publishes are these: Home Health Agency $500 - OIG License Fee Schedule for Other Health Care Facilities, revised 10/15/2026 Beyond the published figures, the operating costs (clinical payroll before certification, insurance, and the reserve operating funds required by 42 CFR 489.28) are larger than every fee on the list.

What do I need to start a home health agency in Kentucky?

The Home Health Agency license (Kentucky Cabinet for Health and Family Services (CHFS), Office of Inspector General (OIG), Division of Health Care), an administrator and clinical leadership who meet Kentucky rules, policies written to the Conditions of Participation at 42 CFR Part 484, skilled nursing plus at least one other therapeutic service, OASIS transmission capability, and, before the federal survey, 10 skilled patients served with 7 receiving care. Medicaid enrollment is a separate application after licensure.

How do I get a home health license in Kentucky?

Apply to the Kentucky Cabinet for Health and Family Services (CHFS), Office of Inspector General (OIG), Division of Health Care under 902 KAR 20:081 (home health agency licensure regulation cited in CHFS and Kentucky regulation references); 907 KAR 1:030 (home health agency services for Kentucky Medicaid). The steps on this page follow the sequence the authority publishes. Confirm the current application and fee on the authority site before you file, because fees and forms are updated on the state calendar.
