How to Start a Group Home in Louisiana

By Dr. Devon Boswell, DBA · November 15, 2026

There is no single group home license in Louisiana. There is a license for each population the state serves, and the population you choose decides the authority, the rule chapter, and the funding stream. This page lists every residential type Louisiana licenses, who runs each one, and the order the application actually runs in.

The group home types Louisiana licenses

Adult Residential Care Provider (ARCP), Levels 1 to 4

Licensed by: Louisiana Department of Health, Health Standards Section. Serves: Two or more adults unrelated to the licensee or operator who receive adult residential care for compensation, including lodging, meals, medication administration, intermittent nursing services, assistance with personal hygiene, transfers and ambulation, dressing, housekeeping and laundry - R.S. 40:2166.3 as quoted on the LDH ARCP page. Standards at LAC 48:I Chapter 68, under R.S. 40:2166.1 to 2166.8. Level 1 is the personal care home level: 2 to 8 residents in a setting designed similarly to a single-family dwelling. Level 2 is the shelter care level: 9 to 16 residents in a congregate setting without independent apartments with kitchenettes. Level 3 is the assisted living level: 17 or more residents in independent apartments equipped with kitchenettes. Level 4: 17 or more residents in independent apartments with kitchenettes, and Level 4 may include intermittent nursing services.

Intermediate Care Facility for Individuals with Intellectual Disabilities (ICF/DD, also ICF/IID)

Licensed by: Louisiana Department of Health, Health Standards Section. Serves: Individuals who require 24 hours of Active Treatment, in community, group, or residential home settings - LDH ICF/DD page. A facility licensed to provide residential care for four or more individuals who meet the criteria for 24 hours per day of Active Treatment. LDH page also cites the federal definitions at 42 CFR 483.102 and 42 CFR 435.1010 for intellectual disability and institutions for individuals with intellectual disabilities or related conditions.

Therapeutic Group Home (TGH)

Licensed by: Louisiana Department of Health, Health Standards Section. Serves: Clients under the age of 21 who are determined to need psychiatric or psychological services - LDH Therapeutic Group Homes page. Community-based residential services in a home-like setting of no greater than 10 beds, under the supervision and oversight of a psychiatrist or psychologist. Provides a structured living environment 24 hours per day, seven days per week, with clinical treatment, psychiatric supports, integration with community resources and skill building. Licensing process is at LAC 48:I sections 6207 and 6209 as referenced on the LDH page.

Community Home and Group Home for persons with developmental disabilities

Licensed by: Louisiana Department of Health, Facility Need Review program definitions at LAC 48:I Chapter 125; services linked to the Office for Citizens with Developmental Disabilities (OCDD) waiver system. Serves: Persons with developmental disabilities served in community residential facilities. FNR definitions in the LDH Title 48 text: a Community Home is a type of community residential facility which has a capacity of eight or fewer beds; a Group Home is a type of community residential facility which has a capacity of nine to 15 beds. Capacity is stated in accordance with R.S. 28:451.2 or current law in the updated FNR text.

Home and Community-Based Services (HCBS) residential modules - Supervised Independent Living and Substitute Family Care

Licensed by: Louisiana Department of Health, Health Standards Section for the HCBS provider license; OCDD for the Residential Options Waiver. Serves: Persons with developmental disabilities receiving waiver residential supports, including shared living. LDH Medicaid Residential Options Waiver (ROW) provider chapter states waiver providers must meet all requirements for licensure established by LDH Health Standards Section, and lists shared living through Supervised Independent Living and Substitute Family Care (host home, adult) among the residential provider types that professionals and agencies may link to. ROW is the 1915(c) waiver named in that LDH Medicaid chapter for these residential options.

How to start a group home in Louisiana, step by step

  1. For an ARCP, LDH shall not process any application until all completed forms, required applicable accompanying information, and the application fee, where required, are received - LDH ARCP page, Licensure Requirements
  2. For a Therapeutic Group Home, the applicant must obtain all inspections and approvals by local agencies, including local zoning boards and ordinances, and submit the TGH application packet with the application form and all additional documentation required by the TGH regulations - LDH Therapeutic Group Homes page
  3. For types subject to Facility Need Review, first receive FNR approval before applying to be licensed by LDH - LAC 48:I Chapter 125 general provisions in LDH Title 48 text

How long does it take in Louisiana?

  • A Therapeutic Group Home must submit a completed license renewal application packet to LDH at least 30 days prior to expiration of the current license - LDH Therapeutic Group Homes page

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to get a group home license in Louisiana?

A Therapeutic Group Home must submit a completed license renewal application packet to LDH at least 30 days prior to expiration of the current license - LDH Therapeutic Group Homes page

What are the requirements to open a group home in Louisiana?

The license for your population from the authority named on this page, a home that passes health and life safety inspection with local zoning cleared, a qualified administrator or manager, background checks on owners and every staff member, written policies matched to the rule chapter, and staffing that matches the residents' assessed needs. Funded placements require separate Medicaid waiver provider enrollment after the license.

How do I get licensed for a group home in Louisiana?

Apply to the authority that licenses homes for your chosen population, listed with each home type on this page. The application, background checks, home inspection, and life safety survey all run through that authority, in the order the steps above follow.
