How to Start a Home Health Agency in Missouri

By Dr. Devon Boswell, DBA · November 15, 2026

Missouri licenses home health agencies as a Home Health Agency license, through the Missouri Department of Health and Senior Services (DHSS), Bureau of Home Care and Rehabilitative Standards, Division of Regulation and Licensure. The governing law is RSMo sections 197.400 to 197.478 and 19 CSR 30-26.010 Home Health Licensure Rule, which incorporates by reference 42 CFR 484 Medicare Conditions of Participation for Home Health Agencies. That state license is the first gate. Medicare certification is the second, and it runs on federal rules that are the same in every state: the Conditions of Participation at 42 CFR Part 484, the Form CMS-855A enrollment, and an initial survey of an agency that is already operating. This page gives you the Missouri license in its own terms, then the federal layer in the order it actually runs.

One status every new owner has to plan around: CMS has a nationwide moratorium on new home health agency Medicare enrollment, imposed May 13, 2026 and extended on November 12, 2026 to about mid-May 2027. The Missouri license work below can proceed now. The federal enrollment cannot finish while the moratorium runs, so sequence your spending accordingly.

How to start a home health agency in Missouri, step by step

  1. Contact the Bureau of Home Care and Rehabilitative Standards to receive the required application and forms for the initial application process (per the DHSS how to apply memo)
  2. Submit required documentation first to the Bureau of Home Care and Rehabilitative Standards; skilled agencies provide physician ordered services on an hourly or intermittent basis
  3. Provide DHSS with a completed application for a home health license, a copy of registration with the Secretary of State, a completed State Disclosure of Ownership and Control Interest Statement form, and sufficient evidence that the agency has established appropriate policies and procedures for providing home health services under RSMo sections 197.400 to 197.478, with the nonrefundable licensure fee
  4. Establish a business location, not in a private residence, with established business hours
  5. Receive deemed status through a CMS approved accrediting organization; DHSS requires accrediting organizations to review prospective agencies for federal regulations and for Missouri state regulations when performing the initial home health survey
  6. Complete the DHSS inspection process; management must allow department representatives to survey the agency to determine eligibility for licensing or renewal, and on-site surveys may be unannounced

Missouri home health license fees and costs

  • Annual renewal: $600 nonrefundable license fee (19 CSR 30-26.010 annual renewal process)
  • The initial licensure fee must accompany the application and is nonrefundable under 19 CSR 30-26.010; the rule text fetched for this research does not state the initial amount, so no initial amount is listed here

How long does it take in Missouri?

  • A home health agency license is renewed annually on department approval when the renewal conditions in 19 CSR 30-26.010 are met
  • For a change of ownership, the owner shall apply for a new license at least 90 calendar days prior to the effective date of sale, transfer or change in corporate status (19 CSR 30-26.010)

What catches Missouri applicants

  • Missouri licensed home health agencies must strictly meet the currently applicable Medicare Conditions of Participation, and surveys for state licensure will be conducted per Medicare standards, per 19 CSR 30-26.010
  • Non-skilled Medicaid personal care and chores services, commonly called In-Home care, are a separate route through the Missouri Department of Social Services, Missouri Medicaid Audit and Compliance, by contract proposal, not through the DHSS home health license (per the DHSS how to apply memo)
  • Certificate of need: the DHSS home health application memo and the home health licensure rule list no certificate of need step for a home health agency. Missouri certificate of need law at RSMo sections 197.300 to 197.366 applies by its terms to new institutional health services and, in the DHSS law page fetched, to beds in facilities licensed under chapter 198 and long-term care hospital beds, with a $600,000 expenditure minimum for capital expenditures for those beds

The Medicare layer, after the Missouri license

With the license in hand and the agency operating, the federal sequence is: build to at least 10 skilled patients served with 7 receiving care, file Form CMS-855A through PECOS and pay the $750 institutional enrollment fee (2026), then pass the initial certification survey, through the state survey agency or an accrediting organization with deeming authority (ACHC, CHAP, or The Joint Commission). The full federal walk-through is on Medicare Certification for Home Health Agencies, and the national path is on How to Start a Home Health Agency.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to start a home health agency in Missouri?

The figures Missouri publishes are these: Annual renewal: $600 nonrefundable license fee (19 CSR 30-26.010 annual renewal process) The initial licensure fee must accompany the application and is nonrefundable under 19 CSR 30-26.010; the rule text fetched for this research does not state the initial amount, so no initial amount is listed here Beyond the published figures, the operating costs (clinical payroll before certification, insurance, and the reserve operating funds required by 42 CFR 489.28) are larger than every fee on the list.

How long does it take to get a home health license in Missouri?

A home health agency license is renewed annually on department approval when the renewal conditions in 19 CSR 30-26.010 are met For a change of ownership, the owner shall apply for a new license at least 90 calendar days prior to the effective date of sale, transfer or change in corporate status (19 CSR 30-26.010) The federal certification that follows runs on the survey calendar, and new Medicare enrollment is under a CMS moratorium to about mid-May 2027.

What do I need to start a home health agency in Missouri?

The Home Health Agency license (Missouri Department of Health and Senior Services (DHSS), Bureau of Home Care and Rehabilitative Standards, Division of Regulation and Licensure), an administrator and clinical leadership who meet Missouri rules, policies written to the Conditions of Participation at 42 CFR Part 484, skilled nursing plus at least one other therapeutic service, OASIS transmission capability, and, before the federal survey, 10 skilled patients served with 7 receiving care. Medicaid enrollment is a separate application after licensure.

How do I get a home health license in Missouri?

Apply to the Missouri Department of Health and Senior Services (DHSS), Bureau of Home Care and Rehabilitative Standards, Division of Regulation and Licensure under RSMo sections 197.400 to 197.478 and 19 CSR 30-26.010 Home Health Licensure Rule, which incorporates by reference 42 CFR 484 Medicare Conditions of Participation for Home Health Agencies. The steps on this page follow the sequence the authority publishes. Confirm the current application and fee on the authority site before you file, because fees and forms are updated on the state calendar.
