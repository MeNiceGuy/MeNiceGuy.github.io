How to Start a Home Health Agency in Pennsylvania

By Dr. Devon Boswell, DBA · November 15, 2026

Pennsylvania licenses home health agencies as a Home Health Care Agency license, called the Home Health Agency license in the initial application packet, through the Pennsylvania Department of Health, Bureau of Community Program Licensure and Certification, Division of Home Health. The governing law is Chapter 8 of the Health Care Facilities Act, 35 P.S. section 448.101 et seq., cited in the initial application as the basis for operating a Home Health Agency; regulations at 28 Pa. Code Chapter 601, Home Health Care Agencies. The separate non-skilled home care track is 28 Pa. Code Chapter 611. That state license is the first gate. Medicare certification is the second, and it runs on federal rules that are the same in every state: the Conditions of Participation at 42 CFR Part 484, the Form CMS-855A enrollment, and an initial survey of an agency that is already operating. This page gives you the Pennsylvania license in its own terms, then the federal layer in the order it actually runs.

One status every new owner has to plan around: CMS has a nationwide moratorium on new home health agency Medicare enrollment, imposed May 13, 2026 and extended on November 12, 2026 to about mid-May 2027. The Pennsylvania license work below can proceed now. The federal enrollment cannot finish while the moratorium runs, so sequence your spending accordingly.

How to start a home health agency in Pennsylvania, step by step

  1. Confirm the agency meets the Department of Health definition at 28 Pa. Code section 601.6: an organization staffed and equipped to provide skilled nursing care and at least one other therapeutic service, home health aides, physical therapy, speech therapy, occupational therapy or medical social services, on a part-time or intermittent basis in the persons place of residence
  2. Submit the Initial Home Health Agency application materials: applications may be emailed to the Division of Home Health initial application address printed on the Department page, and the required fee must be physically mailed for the application to be reviewed
  3. Submit the documentation required for licensure approval, which the Division reviews for substantial compliance with 28 Pa. Code Chapter 601; the packet states the agency cannot admit or treat any patients until approval is received from the Department of Health
  4. If the application is in accordance with Pennsylvania home health agency rules and regulations, the Division issues the license, by email under the current packet
  5. For federal Medicare certification, after state licensure: submit CMS Form 1572(a), CMS Form 690 and CMS Form 1561 to the Division of Home Health, submit CMS Form 855A to the fiscal intermediary, and submit the Federal Civil Rights information for Medicare applicants
  6. After review and acceptance of the application materials and notification from CMS of approval of the CMS-855A, the Department conducts a survey before recommending the agency for Medicare certification

Pennsylvania home health license fees and costs

  • Initial Home Health Agency application, revised February 4, 2025: the regular fee per license is $250, payable by check or money order to the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
  • 28 Pa. Code section 601.11(b) as published in the regulation text fetched states application or renewal forms shall be accompanied by a fee of $50; the current initial application packet amount above is the amount the packet instructs new applicants to pay
  • Home care agency, Chapter 611, separate track: $100 per location, from the published Boswell Consulting Agency article provided for this task

What catches Pennsylvania applicants

  • State licensure as a home health agency is a requirement for federal certification, stated in capital letters on the Pennsylvania Department of Health home health licensure page
  • No fixed licensure timeline is printed: the Department page and the packet both state the length of time for the licensure process depends upon the accuracy of information provided, so no timeline is stated here
  • No certificate of need requirement for a home health agency is printed on the Department licensure page or in the initial application packet fetched
  • CMS approved accreditation organizations listed by the Department for this process are the Joint Commission, ACHC and CHAP

The Medicare layer, after the Pennsylvania license

With the license in hand and the agency operating, the federal sequence is: build to at least 10 skilled patients served with 7 receiving care, file Form CMS-855A through PECOS and pay the $750 institutional enrollment fee (2026), then pass the initial certification survey, through the state survey agency or an accrediting organization with deeming authority (ACHC, CHAP, or The Joint Commission). The full federal walk-through is on Medicare Certification for Home Health Agencies, and the national path is on How to Start a Home Health Agency.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to start a home health agency in Pennsylvania?

The figures Pennsylvania publishes are these: Initial Home Health Agency application, revised February 4, 2025: the regular fee per license is $250, payable by check or money order to the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania 28 Pa. Code section 601.11(b) as published in the regulation text fetched states application or renewal forms shall be accompanied by a fee of $50; the current initial application packet amount above is the amount the packet instructs new applicants to pay Home care agency, Chapter 611, separate track: $100 per location, from the published Boswell Consulting Agency article provided for this task Beyond the published figures, the operating costs (clinical payroll before certification, insurance, and the reserve operating funds required by 42 CFR 489.28) are larger than every fee on the list.

What do I need to start a home health agency in Pennsylvania?

The Home Health Care Agency license, called the Home Health Agency license in the initial application packet (Pennsylvania Department of Health, Bureau of Community Program Licensure and Certification, Division of Home Health), an administrator and clinical leadership who meet Pennsylvania rules, policies written to the Conditions of Participation at 42 CFR Part 484, skilled nursing plus at least one other therapeutic service, OASIS transmission capability, and, before the federal survey, 10 skilled patients served with 7 receiving care. Medicaid enrollment is a separate application after licensure.

How do I get a home health license in Pennsylvania?

Apply to the Pennsylvania Department of Health, Bureau of Community Program Licensure and Certification, Division of Home Health under Chapter 8 of the Health Care Facilities Act, 35 P.S. section 448.101 et seq., cited in the initial application as the basis for operating a Home Health Agency; regulations at 28 Pa. Code Chapter 601, Home Health Care Agencies. The separate non-skilled home care track is 28 Pa. Code Chapter 611. The steps on this page follow the sequence the authority publishes. Confirm the current application and fee on the authority site before you file, because fees and forms are updated on the state calendar.
