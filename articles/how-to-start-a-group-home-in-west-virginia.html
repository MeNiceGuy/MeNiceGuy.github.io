How to Start a Group Home in West Virginia

By Dr. Devon Boswell, DBA · November 15, 2026

There is no single group home license in West Virginia. There is a license for each population the state serves, and the population you choose decides the authority, the rule chapter, and the funding stream. This page lists every residential type West Virginia licenses, who runs each one, and the order the application actually runs in.

The group home types West Virginia licenses

Assisted Living Residence

Licensed by: OHFLAC, Office of Inspector General. Serves: Four or more residents who are dependent upon the services of others by reason of physical or mental impairment and who may need no more than limited and intermittent nursing care; small residences are 4 to 16 beds and large residences are 17 or more beds in the definitions summarized from W. Va. Code 16B-5. Licensed under W. Va. Code Chapter 16B, Article 5, formerly 16-5D, with rules recodified from 64 CSR 14. No person may operate an assisted living residence without first obtaining a license. OHFLAC fee schedule effective June 1, 2025 lists Assisted Living Residences at $65 initial and $10.16 per bed renewal; the June 1, 2026 fee order lists $65 and $10.42 per bed. The Aged and Disabled Waiver manual, BMS Chapter 501 effective June 1, 2026, includes a section 501.16 on Assisted Living Residences and Group Residential Facilities.

Residential Care Community

Licensed by: OHFLAC, Office of Inspector General. Serves: 17 or more persons who are or may be dependent upon the services of others by reason of physical or mental impairment or who may require limited and intermittent nursing care but who are capable of self preservation and are not bedfast, per W. Va. Code 16B-9-2. A group of 17 or more residential apartments that are part of a larger independent living community, providing residential accommodations, personal assistance and supervision on a monthly basis. Each apartment shall be at least 300 square feet, have lockable doors, and contain at least one bedroom, one kitchenette with a sink and refrigerator, and one full bathroom. Licensed under W. Va. Code Chapter 16B, Article 9. Fee schedule lists $75 initial and $7.57 per bed renewal effective June 1, 2025, and $7.76 per bed in the June 1, 2026 order.

Legally Unlicensed Health Care Home

Licensed by: OHFLAC, Office of Inspector General - registration, not licensure. Serves: One to three residents who are not related to the operator by blood or marriage within the degree of consanguinity of second cousin, who may be dependent on others by reason of physical or mental impairment or may require limited and intermittent nursing care. Any residence providing accommodations, personal assistance and supervision, whether for compensation or not, for more than 24 hours to one to three such persons must register with OHFLAC, per the OHFLAC Legally Unlicensed form and W. Va. Code 16B-6-3, which states no fee may be charged for registration and a provider may operate no more than one legally unlicensed home. Background checks through WV CARES must be completed before approval as a provider, per the registration instructions.

Behavioral Health Center - residential and group residential services, including IDD Waiver residential agencies

Licensed by: OHFLAC, Office of Inspector General, under Behavioral Health Centers Licensure rule, formerly 64 CSR 11 and recodified as 71 CSR 25 effective May 1, 2026 in the Secretary of State filing. Serves: Consumers receiving behavioral health services and supports, including persons who are developmentally disabled or behaviorally disabled in group residential facilities as defined in legislative text: not more than eight individuals who are developmentally disabled and not more than three supervisors, or not more than twelve individuals who are behaviorally disabled and not more than three supervisors. BMS Chapter 513, Intellectual and Developmental Disabilities Waiver, requires IDDW providers to receive Certificate of Need approval from the West Virginia Health Care Authority and to obtain and maintain a behavioral health license through OHFLAC, except case management only agencies, and to obtain provider certification through the utilization management contractor. The IDD Waiver is the Medicaid waiver that funds these placements in the sources reviewed. OHFLAC fee schedule lists Behavioral Health Facilities at $10 initial and $10 renewal.

Recovery Residence

Licensed by: OHFLAC, Office of Inspector General - registration. Serves: Persons in sustained, long term recovery from substance use disorder, in a single family, drug free and alcohol free residential dwelling unit or other form of group housing, per the OHFLAC recovery residence flyer. All persons operating a recovery residence in West Virginia are required to be registered under state law; registration is separate from certification to operate. To register, complete an application and submit it to OHFLAC with the $250 registration fee, which may be adjusted annually for inflation. Recovery residences must report specified data quarterly to OHFLAC and the Department of Human Services. Fee schedule lists $250 initial and $250 renewal.

How to start a group home in West Virginia, step by step

  1. For a behavioral health center, per the Bureau for Behavioral Health Steps for New Agencies document: apply and receive a Certificate of Need or exemption for any new proposed health care service through the West Virginia Health Care Authority
  2. Contact the OHFLAC Life Safety Program for any new construction or renovation; building sites and architectural plans must be approved by OHFLAC before construction, and occupancy must be inspected and approved before occupancy is granted; submit a Pre Opening Inspection Request form 2 to 3 weeks before the desired occupancy date
  3. Once the construction completion date is certain and policies and procedures are developed, submit a Behavioral Health Services Application for Licensure to OHFLAC; the center must be in substantial compliance with the Behavioral Health Centers Licensure state rule
  4. After the State Fire Marshal recommendation is received, OHFLAC contacts the applicant to schedule the initial licensure survey
  5. For an assisted living residence, submit a completed initial licensure application to OHFLAC with the nonrefundable fee and any additional information the Secretary may require, per the assisted living rule text at 64 CSR 14, section 3.2

West Virginia group home license fees and costs

  • Assisted Living Residences: $65 initial and $10.16 per bed renewal in the OHFLAC fee schedule effective June 1, 2025
  • Residential Care Communities: $75 initial and $7.57 per bed renewal in the same schedule
  • Behavioral Health Facilities: $10 initial and $10 renewal in the same schedule
  • Recovery Residences: $250 initial and $250 renewal in the same schedule, and $250 registration fee in the OHFLAC recovery residence flyer

How long does it take in West Virginia?

  • Behavioral health center Pre Opening Inspection Request form must be submitted to the OHFLAC Life Safety Program 2 to 3 weeks prior to the date the applicant would like to occupy the building, per the Steps for New Agencies document
  • Assisted living license is issued for a period not to exceed one year in the rule text summarized at 64 CSR 14, section 3.1.6

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to open a group home in West Virginia?

The figures West Virginia publishes are these: Assisted Living Residences: $65 initial and $10.16 per bed renewal in the OHFLAC fee schedule effective June 1, 2025 Residential Care Communities: $75 initial and $7.57 per bed renewal in the same schedule Behavioral Health Facilities: $10 initial and $10 renewal in the same schedule Recovery Residences: $250 initial and $250 renewal in the same schedule, and $250 registration fee in the OHFLAC recovery residence flyer The license fee is rarely the largest cost. The home itself, life safety and accessibility work, insurance, and staffing hired before the first placement carry the project.

How long does it take to get a group home license in West Virginia?

Behavioral health center Pre Opening Inspection Request form must be submitted to the OHFLAC Life Safety Program 2 to 3 weeks prior to the date the applicant would like to occupy the building, per the Steps for New Agencies document Assisted living license is issued for a period not to exceed one year in the rule text summarized at 64 CSR 14, section 3.1.6

What are the requirements to open a group home in West Virginia?

The license for your population from the authority named on this page, a home that passes health and life safety inspection with local zoning cleared, a qualified administrator or manager, background checks on owners and every staff member, written policies matched to the rule chapter, and staffing that matches the residents' assessed needs. Funded placements require separate Medicaid waiver provider enrollment after the license.

How do I get licensed for a group home in West Virginia?

Apply to the authority that licenses homes for your chosen population, listed with each home type on this page. The application, background checks, home inspection, and life safety survey all run through that authority, in the order the steps above follow.
