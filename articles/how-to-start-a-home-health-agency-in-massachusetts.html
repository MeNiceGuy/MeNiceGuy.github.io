How to Start a Home Health Agency in Massachusetts

By Dr. Devon Boswell, DBA · November 15, 2026

Massachusetts licenses home health agencies as a Home Health Agency license under M.G.L. c.111 Section 51K (3-year term), and, for billing, Medicare certification as a home health agency through DPH, through the Massachusetts Department of Public Health (DPH), Division of Health Care Facility Licensure and Certification - for Medicare certification; the home health agency license in M.G.L. c.111 Section 51K is a Department of Public Health license. The governing law is M.G.L. c.111 Section 51K (home health agency licensure; fees established pursuant to Section 3B of chapter 7, no amount printed in the statute); federal Medicare home health regulations at 42 CFR Part 484 as listed on the DPH certification page. That state license is the first gate. Medicare certification is the second, and it runs on federal rules that are the same in every state: the Conditions of Participation at 42 CFR Part 484, the Form CMS-855A enrollment, and an initial survey of an agency that is already operating. This page gives you the Massachusetts license in its own terms, then the federal layer in the order it actually runs.

One status every new owner has to plan around: CMS has a nationwide moratorium on new home health agency Medicare enrollment, imposed May 13, 2026 and extended on November 12, 2026 to about mid-May 2027. The Massachusetts license work below can proceed now. The federal enrollment cannot finish while the moratorium runs, so sequence your spending accordingly.

How to start a home health agency in Massachusetts, step by step

  1. For Medicare certification, complete all steps in the DPH HHA Application Instructions
  2. Compile the required documents in this order: Home Health Agency Medicare Deemed Status Certification Checklist (cover sheet); CMS-1561 Health Insurance Benefit Agreement (3 copies, fresh-ink originals); HHS-690 Assurance of Compliance; OMB 0990-2043 Civil Rights Information Request Form; a deemed status letter from a Medicare-approved accrediting agency stating the agency meets Medicare Conditions of Participation and the effective date; CMS-1572 Home Health Agency Survey and Deficiencies Report (omit numbers 7, 8, 11, 21, 22)
  3. Mail the request to the Licensure Coordinator, Department of Public Health, Division of Health Care Facility Licensure and Certification, 67 Forest Street, Marlborough, MA 01752
  4. DPH processes the materials to the CMS Regional Office, which assigns a Medicare provider number
  5. For MassHealth (Medicaid) enrollment, the agency must first be certified as an HHA in the Medicare program, then contact MassHealth Provider Enrollment

How long does it take in Massachusetts?

  • Home health agency license term under M.G.L. c.111 Section 51K: 3 years, renewable for a like term

What catches Massachusetts applicants

  • Verification conflict, flagged rather than resolved: the current text of M.G.L. c.111 Section 51K on malegislature.gov states no person shall establish, maintain, operate or hold oneself out as a home health agency without a home health agency license issued by the department, for a term of 3 years, and that a Medicare or Medicaid certified agency is deemed to meet the requirements of the initial licensure application. The DPH Medicare certification page fetched on the same day still states the opposite for opening a business: you do not have to apply to the Department to open a home health business and Massachusetts laws for DPH do not include licensure of home health agencies. No DPH home health license application form or fee amount was found on the DPH pages fetched, so no fee is listed
  • Under Section 51K, DPH may impose a fine of up to $10,000 on a person or entity that operates or advertises a home health agency without a license
  • Medicare program facts from the DPH page: the agency must provide skilled nursing and at least one other qualifying service (physical therapy, occupational therapy, speech/language pathology, medical social services, home health aides), and agency employees must provide at least one qualifying service
  • Separate non-medical track, not home health: Chapter 180 of the Acts of 2026, approved August 6, 2026, enacted the first Massachusetts home care agency license, at M.G.L. c.6A Sections 16HH to 16KK, issued by the Executive Office of Health and Human Services for a term of 3 years, with fees established under Section 3B of chapter 7 and no application open yet in the sources fetched. The act orders temporary licensure regulations and temporary licenses within 180 days of its effective date, final regulations within 1 year of the effective date, and a compliance date not later than 1 year after those regulations are adopted. Chapter 180 expressly excludes a home health agency as defined in Section 51K of chapter 111 from the home care agency definition

The Medicare layer, after the Massachusetts license

With the license in hand and the agency operating, the federal sequence is: build to at least 10 skilled patients served with 7 receiving care, file Form CMS-855A through PECOS and pay the $750 institutional enrollment fee (2026), then pass the initial certification survey, through the state survey agency or an accrediting organization with deeming authority (ACHC, CHAP, or The Joint Commission). The full federal walk-through is on Medicare Certification for Home Health Agencies, and the national path is on How to Start a Home Health Agency.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to get a home health license in Massachusetts?

Home health agency license term under M.G.L. c.111 Section 51K: 3 years, renewable for a like term The federal certification that follows runs on the survey calendar, and new Medicare enrollment is under a CMS moratorium to about mid-May 2027.

What do I need to start a home health agency in Massachusetts?

The Home Health Agency license under M.G.L. c.111 Section 51K (3-year term), and, for billing, Medicare certification as a home health agency through DPH (Massachusetts Department of Public Health (DPH), Division of Health Care Facility Licensure and Certification - for Medicare certification; the home health agency license in M.G.L. c.111 Section 51K is a Department of Public Health license), an administrator and clinical leadership who meet Massachusetts rules, policies written to the Conditions of Participation at 42 CFR Part 484, skilled nursing plus at least one other therapeutic service, OASIS transmission capability, and, before the federal survey, 10 skilled patients served with 7 receiving care. Medicaid enrollment is a separate application after licensure.

How do I get a home health license in Massachusetts?

Apply to the Massachusetts Department of Public Health (DPH), Division of Health Care Facility Licensure and Certification - for Medicare certification; the home health agency license in M.G.L. c.111 Section 51K is a Department of Public Health license under M.G.L. c.111 Section 51K (home health agency licensure; fees established pursuant to Section 3B of chapter 7, no amount printed in the statute); federal Medicare home health regulations at 42 CFR Part 484 as listed on the DPH certification page. The steps on this page follow the sequence the authority publishes. Confirm the current application and fee on the authority site before you file, because fees and forms are updated on the state calendar.
