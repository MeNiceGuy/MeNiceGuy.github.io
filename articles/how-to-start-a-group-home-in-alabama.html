How to Start a Group Home in Alabama

By Dr. Devon Boswell, DBA · November 15, 2026

There is no single group home license in Alabama. There is a license for each population the state serves, and the population you choose decides the authority, the rule chapter, and the funding stream. This page lists every residential type Alabama licenses, who runs each one, and the order the application actually runs in.

The group home types Alabama licenses

Assisted Living Facility

Licensed by: Alabama Department of Public Health, Bureau of Health Provider Standards. Serves: Three or more individuals who are in need of assistance with activities of daily living. ADPH definition: an entity that provides or offers to provide residence and personal care to three or more individuals in need of assistance with activities of daily living. Initial license fee is $240 plus $18 for each bed, and fees are not refundable. A license may be granted on approval of the application, building approval from Technical Services and a successful on-site survey. It is a violation of state law to provide assisted living facility services before a license is granted.

Specialty Care Assisted Living Facility (SCALF)

Licensed by: Alabama Department of Public Health, Bureau of Health Provider Standards. Serves: Residents with a degree of cognitive impairment that would ordinarily make them ineligible for admission or continued stay in an assisted living facility. A facility that meets the Assisted Living Facility definition but is specially licensed and staffed to care for residents with that degree of cognitive impairment. Initial application fee is $240 plus $18 for each bed. The SCALF application list includes a Certificate of Need obtainable from SHPDA. Separate license certificates are required for a building that will house both specialty care assisted living beds and regular assisted living beds.

Adult Foster Care homes (approved homes)

Licensed by: Alabama Department of Human Resources (DHR). Serves: Individuals 18 years of age or older who, because of physical, mental or emotional limitations, are unable to live alone and who have no families with whom they can live. DHR describes Adult Foster Care as service provided in approved homes on a 24 hour basis for eligible adults 18 and older. DHR directs interested persons to complete an online inquiry form for more information. No license fee or bed band was verified from a DHR page fetched for this research.

Group home and other residential habilitation for persons with intellectual disabilities

Licensed by: Alabama Department of Mental Health (ADMH), Division of Developmental Disabilities, for provider certification; Alabama Medicaid Agency for the Intellectual Disabilities Waiver. Serves: Individuals age three and above diagnosed as intellectually disabled who meet the income, resource, disability or age and medical criteria for an intermediate care facility for individuals with intellectual disabilities. Alabama Medicaid lists Intellectual Disabilities Waiver services including residential habilitation, group home or other, and day habilitation, and directs applicants to contact the Department of Mental Health. ADMH states that by Alabama law any community program providing services to people living with developmental disabilities, mental illnesses or substance use disorders must be certified by ADMH prior to providing services, through a multi-phase orientation and application process demonstrating compliance with Alabama Administrative Code standards. Effective October 31, 2025, prospective DD and mental health and substance use providers submit the certification application through the automated ACAP system.

Psychiatric Residential Treatment Facility (PRTF)

Licensed by: Alabama Department of Public Health facility description; Medicaid enrolled psychiatric facility as described by ADPH. Serves: Residents under the age of 21 who need inpatient psychiatric treatment under the direction of a physician. ADPH describes a PRTF as a psychiatric hospital enrolled as a Medicaid provider or an accredited psychiatric residential treatment facility that provides a range of comprehensive services to treat the psychiatric condition of residents under age 21 on an inpatient basis under the direction of a physician, with the purpose of improving the resident condition or preventing further regression so services will no longer be needed.

How to start a group home in Alabama, step by step

  1. For an Assisted Living Facility or SCALF: submit the initial license application to ADPH with the $240 plus $18 per bed fee and the documents the application lists, including administrator license, medical director license and required policies and procedures, and for a SCALF a Certificate of Need from SHPDA
  2. For an Assisted Living Facility or SCALF: obtain building approval from ADPH Technical Services and pass the on-site pre-licensure survey; a license may be granted on approval of the application, building approval and a successful on-site survey
  3. For ADMH certified community programs including developmental disability residential services: complete the required two phase prospective provider orientation, read the Alabama Administrative Code standards for the service area, and submit the certification application, through ACAP for DD and mental health and substance use services from October 31, 2025
  4. For Adult Foster Care: complete the DHR online inquiry form for more information, per the DHR adult services page

Alabama group home license fees and costs

  • Assisted Living Facility initial, renewal and change of ownership: $240 plus $18 for each bed
  • Specialty Care Assisted Living Facility initial, renewal and change of ownership: $240 plus $18 for each bed
  • Health care facility late penalty for annual renewal, as listed by ADPH for licensed facilities generally: $300 or 100 percent of the renewal fee, whichever is greater

How long does it take in Alabama?

  • Assisted Living Facility application review takes a minimum of thirty days, and a review of the policies and procedures required by the Rules of the Alabama State Board of Health for Assisted Living Facilities is required before the license is granted (per the ADPH initial ALF application)

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to open a group home in Alabama?

The figures Alabama publishes are these: Assisted Living Facility initial, renewal and change of ownership: $240 plus $18 for each bed Specialty Care Assisted Living Facility initial, renewal and change of ownership: $240 plus $18 for each bed Health care facility late penalty for annual renewal, as listed by ADPH for licensed facilities generally: $300 or 100 percent of the renewal fee, whichever is greater The license fee is rarely the largest cost. The home itself, life safety and accessibility work, insurance, and staffing hired before the first placement carry the project.

How long does it take to get a group home license in Alabama?

Assisted Living Facility application review takes a minimum of thirty days, and a review of the policies and procedures required by the Rules of the Alabama State Board of Health for Assisted Living Facilities is required before the license is granted (per the ADPH initial ALF application)

What are the requirements to open a group home in Alabama?

The license for your population from the authority named on this page, a home that passes health and life safety inspection with local zoning cleared, a qualified administrator or manager, background checks on owners and every staff member, written policies matched to the rule chapter, and staffing that matches the residents' assessed needs. Funded placements require separate Medicaid waiver provider enrollment after the license.

How do I get licensed for a group home in Alabama?

Apply to the authority that licenses homes for your chosen population, listed with each home type on this page. The application, background checks, home inspection, and life safety survey all run through that authority, in the order the steps above follow.
