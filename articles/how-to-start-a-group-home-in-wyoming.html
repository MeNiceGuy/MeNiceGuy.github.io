How to Start a Group Home in Wyoming

By Dr. Devon Boswell, DBA · November 15, 2026

There is no single group home license in Wyoming. There is a license for each population the state serves, and the population you choose decides the authority, the rule chapter, and the funding stream. This page lists every residential type Wyoming licenses, who runs each one, and the order the application actually runs in.

The group home types Wyoming licenses

Assisted Living Facility

Licensed by: Wyoming Department of Health, Healthcare Licensing and Surveys (HLS). Serves: Persons not related to the owner who need limited nursing care, personal care, and boarding home care. A dwelling operated by any person, firm, or corporation engaged in providing limited nursing care, personal care, and boarding home care, but not habilitative care, for persons not related to the owner of the facility. The definition may include facilities with secured units and facilities dedicated to the special care and services for people with Alzheimer's disease or other dementia conditions (Wyo. Stat. section 35-2-901, as reproduced in WDH materials). Medicaid home and community based help runs through the Community Choices Waiver, which assists older adults age 65 and over on or eligible for Medicaid and individuals ages 19 to 64 with a confirmed disability meeting Social Security Administration criteria (WDH HCBS page).

Boarding Home

Licensed by: Wyoming Department of Health, Healthcare Licensing and Surveys (HLS). Serves: Persons not related to the owner who need rooms, meals, and personal daily living care, without nursing or habilitative care. A dwelling or rooming house operated by any person, firm, or corporation engaged in the business of operating a home for the purpose of letting rooms for rental, and providing meals and personal daily living care, but not habilitative or nursing care, for persons not related to the owner. Boarding home does not include a lodging facility or an apartment in which only room and board is provided (HLS Facility Types page).

Adult Foster Care Home

Licensed by: Wyoming Department of Health, Healthcare Licensing and Surveys (HLS). Serves: Up to five adults not related to the provider who need long-term care in a homelike atmosphere. A home where care is provided for up to five adults who are not related to the provider by blood, marriage, or adoption, in need of long-term care in a homelike atmosphere. Resident clients shall have private rooms, which may be shared with spouses, and individual handicapped accessible bathrooms. Adult foster homes does not include any residential facility otherwise licensed or funded by the state of Wyoming (HLS Facility Types page).

Intermediate Care Facility for Individuals with Intellectual Disabilities (ICF/IID)

Licensed by: Wyoming Department of Health, Healthcare Licensing and Surveys (HLS). Serves: Individuals with intellectual disabilities who need health-related care and training on a regular basis. Listed by HLS as a licensed facility type: a facility that provides on a regular basis health-related care and training to individuals with intellectual disabilities (HLS Facility Types page, definition truncated in the page text reviewed). Residential supports for people with intellectual disabilities and acquired brain injury are funded through the Comprehensive Waiver and the Supports Waiver, the Developmental Disability Waivers managed by the WDH HCBS Section; the Comprehensive Waiver requires additional emergency criteria, and the Supports Waiver offers a limited budget for personalized services (WDH HCBS page).

How to start a group home in Wyoming, step by step

  1. On the HLS Facility Types page, click a specific facility type to access licensing information and a license application for that facility type
  2. Submit the application electronically with all required documents, or request the application by email to wdh-ohls@wyo.gov if unable to download it (HLS Facility Types page)
  3. Facility types requiring construction approval include Adult Foster Care, Assisted Living Facility, Boarding Home, and Intermediate Care Facility for Individuals with Intellectual Disabilities (HLS Guidelines to the Chapter 3 Construction Rules)

Frequently asked questions

What are the requirements to open a group home in Wyoming?

The license for your population from the authority named on this page, a home that passes health and life safety inspection with local zoning cleared, a qualified administrator or manager, background checks on owners and every staff member, written policies matched to the rule chapter, and staffing that matches the residents' assessed needs. Funded placements require separate Medicaid waiver provider enrollment after the license.

How do I get licensed for a group home in Wyoming?

Apply to the authority that licenses homes for your chosen population, listed with each home type on this page. The application, background checks, home inspection, and life safety survey all run through that authority, in the order the steps above follow.
