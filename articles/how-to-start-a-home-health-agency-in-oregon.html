How to Start a Home Health Agency in Oregon

By Dr. Devon Boswell, DBA · November 15, 2026

Oregon licenses home health agencies as a Home Health Agency license, through the Oregon Health Authority, Public Health Division, Health Facility Licensing and Certification Program (Health Care Regulation and Quality Improvement). The governing law is Oregon Revised Statutes 443.014 to 443.105; Oregon Administrative Rules Chapter 333, Division 027 (OAR 333-027); federal Medicare regulations at 42 CFR Part 484 and CMS State Operations Manual Appendix B, both listed by OHA for home health agencies. That state license is the first gate. Medicare certification is the second, and it runs on federal rules that are the same in every state: the Conditions of Participation at 42 CFR Part 484, the Form CMS-855A enrollment, and an initial survey of an agency that is already operating. This page gives you the Oregon license in its own terms, then the federal layer in the order it actually runs.

One status every new owner has to plan around: CMS has a nationwide moratorium on new home health agency Medicare enrollment, imposed May 13, 2026 and extended on November 12, 2026 to about mid-May 2027. The Oregon license work below can proceed now. The federal enrollment cannot finish while the moratorium runs, so sequence your spending accordingly.

How to start a home health agency in Oregon, step by step

  1. Complete the Home Health Agency License Application form (OHA application checklist for new agencies)
  2. Complete the Owner and Administrator Background Check Request form or forms if applicable
  3. If in-home care services will be provided under the home health license, complete the Home Health Agency attestation form for provision of In-Home Care Services
  4. Include a check or money order for $4,000 payable to the Oregon Health Authority
  5. Include a resume for the administrator showing the administrator is a physician or registered nurse currently licensed in Oregon with education, experience, and knowledge in community health service systems, or an individual with education, experience, and knowledge in a related community health service system and at least one year of overall administrative experience in home health care or a related community health program
  6. Develop agency-specific policies, procedures, forms, and curriculums demonstrating compliance with the home health OARs, Division 27, including a sampling demonstrating OAR 333-027-0001 Compliance with Federal Law, OAR 333-027-0060 Administration of Home Health Agency, and OAR 333-027-0080 Advance Directives
  7. Send the application packet to Health Care Regulation and Quality Improvement, PO Box 14260, Portland, OR 97293, attention HHA Program; partial or incomplete applications cannot be accepted

Oregon home health license fees and costs

  • New agency license fee $4,000, all application fees non-refundable per OAR 333-027-0010(7) - OHA Home Health Agency License Application, revised 08/2024
  • Annual renewal $2,125 - OHA Home Health Agency License Application fee schedule
  • Change of ownership $1,250 - OHA Home Health Agency License Application fee schedule

How long does it take in Oregon?

  • Renewal application must be submitted at least 30 days prior to the license expiration date, per OAR 333-027-0020 (OHA application form)
  • Agency geographic service area is limited to within a 60-mile radius of the parent location unless a waiver is obtained (OHA application form)

What catches Oregon applicants

  • Oregon licenses home health agencies at the state level through OHA. OHA describes home health agencies as providing skilled medical nursing services and other therapeutic services to patients in their homes.
  • OHA lists separate prospective licensee documents for licensure and for Medicare certification (Certification Information HHA and Initial Letter HHA) on its Health Facility Licensing and Certification page. The application packet fetched does not state that a state license must issue before Medicare certification, so that ordering is not claimed here.
  • No certificate of need statement for home health agencies was printed on the OHA pages fetched, so CON is not claimed either way in this sheet.

The Medicare layer, after the Oregon license

With the license in hand and the agency operating, the federal sequence is: build to at least 10 skilled patients served with 7 receiving care, file Form CMS-855A through PECOS and pay the $750 institutional enrollment fee (2026), then pass the initial certification survey, through the state survey agency or an accrediting organization with deeming authority (ACHC, CHAP, or The Joint Commission). The full federal walk-through is on Medicare Certification for Home Health Agencies, and the national path is on How to Start a Home Health Agency.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to start a home health agency in Oregon?

The figures Oregon publishes are these: New agency license fee $4,000, all application fees non-refundable per OAR 333-027-0010(7) - OHA Home Health Agency License Application, revised 08/2024 Annual renewal $2,125 - OHA Home Health Agency License Application fee schedule Change of ownership $1,250 - OHA Home Health Agency License Application fee schedule Beyond the published figures, the operating costs (clinical payroll before certification, insurance, and the reserve operating funds required by 42 CFR 489.28) are larger than every fee on the list.

How long does it take to get a home health license in Oregon?

Renewal application must be submitted at least 30 days prior to the license expiration date, per OAR 333-027-0020 (OHA application form) Agency geographic service area is limited to within a 60-mile radius of the parent location unless a waiver is obtained (OHA application form) The federal certification that follows runs on the survey calendar, and new Medicare enrollment is under a CMS moratorium to about mid-May 2027.

What do I need to start a home health agency in Oregon?

The Home Health Agency license (Oregon Health Authority, Public Health Division, Health Facility Licensing and Certification Program (Health Care Regulation and Quality Improvement)), an administrator and clinical leadership who meet Oregon rules, policies written to the Conditions of Participation at 42 CFR Part 484, skilled nursing plus at least one other therapeutic service, OASIS transmission capability, and, before the federal survey, 10 skilled patients served with 7 receiving care. Medicaid enrollment is a separate application after licensure.

How do I get a home health license in Oregon?

Apply to the Oregon Health Authority, Public Health Division, Health Facility Licensing and Certification Program (Health Care Regulation and Quality Improvement) under Oregon Revised Statutes 443.014 to 443.105; Oregon Administrative Rules Chapter 333, Division 027 (OAR 333-027); federal Medicare regulations at 42 CFR Part 484 and CMS State Operations Manual Appendix B, both listed by OHA for home health agencies. The steps on this page follow the sequence the authority publishes. Confirm the current application and fee on the authority site before you file, because fees and forms are updated on the state calendar.
