How to Start a Group Home in Iowa

By Dr. Devon Boswell, DBA · November 15, 2026

There is no single group home license in Iowa. There is a license for each population the state serves, and the population you choose decides the authority, the rule chapter, and the funding stream. This page lists every residential type Iowa licenses, who runs each one, and the order the application actually runs in.

The group home types Iowa licenses

Assisted Living Program (ALP)

Licensed by: Iowa Department of Inspections, Appeals, and Licensing (DIAL). Serves: Tenants, three or more, who need housing with services. Certified, not licensed, under Iowa Code Chapter 231C and 481 IAC Chapter 69. Assisted living is housing with services that may include health-related care, personal care, and assistance with instrumental activities of daily living to three or more tenants in a physical structure providing a homelike environment, with 24 hours per day response staff. A dementia-specific assisted living program is a certified ALP meeting the dementia criteria in 481 IAC 69.1. Certification process is at 481 IAC 69.3 to 69.5. Iowa Medicaid Elderly Waiver covers assisted living service for people age 65 and older.

Elder Group Home (EGH)

Licensed by: Iowa Department of Inspections, Appeals, and Licensing (DIAL). Serves: Elders, defined as persons 60 years of age or older, three through five per home. Certified under Iowa Code Chapter 231B and 481 IAC Chapter 68, and the rule states certification is equivalent to licensure. A single-family residence operated by a person providing room, board, and personal care, and which may provide health-related services, to three through five elders not related to the provider within the third degree, staffed by an on-site manager 24 hours per day, seven days per week.

Residential Care Facility (RCF)

Licensed by: Iowa Department of Inspections, Appeals, and Licensing (DIAL). Serves: Three or more individuals who, by reason of illness, disease, or physical or mental infirmity, are unable to sufficiently or properly care for themselves but do not require registered or licensed practical nurse services except on an emergency basis. Licensed health care facility under Iowa Code Chapter 135C, section 135C.1(22). Provides, for a period exceeding 24 consecutive hours, accommodation, board, personal assistance, and other essential daily living activities to three or more individuals not related to the administrator or owner within the third degree. Iowa Code section 135C.4 covers residential care facilities, including special classifications such as residential care facilities for persons with mental illness (481 IAC Chapter 62 in DIAL practice).

Intermediate Care Facility for Persons with an Intellectual Disability (ICF/IID)

Licensed by: Iowa Department of Inspections, Appeals, and Licensing (DIAL). Serves: Three or more individuals who primarily have an intellectual disability or a related condition. Licensed health care facility under Iowa Code Chapter 135C, section 135C.1(10). An institution or distinct part of an institution whose primary purpose is to provide health or rehabilitative services, meeting this chapter and the federal standards for intermediate care facilities for persons with an intellectual disability in 42 CFR Part 483, Subpart D. Iowa also defines an intermediate care facility for persons with mental illness at section 135C.1(11), providing accommodation, board, and nursing care for more than 24 consecutive hours to three or more individuals who primarily have mental illness.

Boarding Home (registered)

Licensed by: Iowa Department of Inspections, Appeals, and Licensing (DIAL), Health Facilities staff. Serves: Three or more individuals who require supervision or assistance completing everyday tasks such as bathing, dressing, using the toilet, and eating. Boarding homes are not considered health facilities but Iowa law requires them to register with DIAL under Iowa Code Chapter 135O, per the DIAL Boarding Homes page. Registration is not a health facility license.

How to start a group home in Iowa, step by step

  1. For an assisted living program or elder group home, complete the application packet obtained from the department, through the DIAL Health Facilities Division website, by mail from DIAL Adult Services Bureau, 6200 Park Avenue, Des Moines, Iowa 50321, or by telephone (481 IAC 69.3 and 68.3)
  2. Submit the completed application and all supporting documentation, with the fee stated in Iowa Code section 231B.17 for an elder group home (481 IAC 68.3(3)); assisted living application content is listed at 481 IAC 69.4, including ownership disclosure and, if the program contracts for personal care or health-related care from a certified home health agency, a copy of that entity current certification or license
  3. For an elder group home, complete the initial certification process at 481 IAC 68.5, which begins on receipt of all completed documentation including state fire marshal approval
  4. Display the current certificate visibly in the program (481 IAC 68.2(2) and 69.2(1))

Frequently asked questions

What are the requirements to open a group home in Iowa?

The license for your population from the authority named on this page, a home that passes health and life safety inspection with local zoning cleared, a qualified administrator or manager, background checks on owners and every staff member, written policies matched to the rule chapter, and staffing that matches the residents' assessed needs. Funded placements require separate Medicaid waiver provider enrollment after the license.

How do I get licensed for a group home in Iowa?

Apply to the authority that licenses homes for your chosen population, listed with each home type on this page. The application, background checks, home inspection, and life safety survey all run through that authority, in the order the steps above follow.
