Services

Your licensing packet, built for you.

I build your complete home care or home health licensing packet for your state and your track, assembled from the same verified components as the Manual and Template Generator, and deliver it complete and in filing order.

The generator is the do-it-yourself tier: you pick your state and track and assemble the packet yourself. This is the done-for-you tier: you answer a short intake by email, and I do the assembly, from the same component library, checked against the same state sources. Written and asynchronous, like everything here. No calls at any point.

The complete packet, in filing order

Checklist

Step One

The state application checklist

Every form, fee, and filing step for your state and track, in the order the state wants them, with the sources shown so you can see where each requirement comes from.

Manual

Step Two

The policies and procedures manual

The full manual for your track, built from the verified policy components for your state: the policies your licensure survey and your payer enrollment will ask for, written to the governing rules.

Payer sequence

Step Three

The payer enrollment sequence

The Medicaid and Medicare enrollment steps that follow licensure for your track, in sequence, including any current enrollment pause or moratorium that applies to your state and track, stated plainly before you plan around it.

Startup set

Step Four

The startup documents

The operating documents that complete the packet: the forms and records a new agency needs in place at filing and at opening, assembled with the manual so the whole set agrees with itself.

How It Works

Purchase, a short email intake, delivery

1

Purchase

Check out on Gumroad

Purchase runs through the Boswell Consulting Agency storefront on Gumroad, where the current price for a standard packet build is shown before you pay. No consultation call and no proposal process come first.

2

Intake

Answer four things by email

After purchase I send a short intake by email. It asks for your state, your track (the care type and payer path you are filing under), your entity status (formed, forming, or not yet started), and your timeline. That is the whole intake.

3

Build

I assemble your packet

I build the packet for your state and track from the verified component library: the checklist, the manual, the payer sequence, and the startup documents, checked against your state's current statutes, rules, and forms, and ordered the way the filing runs.

4

Delivery

You receive the complete packet in writing

Delivery is by email and download, in writing, with the packet complete and in filing order. Follow-up questions about what you received are handled in writing as well.

The Boundary

What this is, and what it is not

Written and Asynchronous

No calls, no meetings, no phone tag

The intake, the build, and the delivery all happen in writing. That is the model for every product and service on this site, and it is why the work does not wait on anyone's calendar.

Your Filing Stays Yours

I build the packet; you file it

I do not submit applications, file forms, or deal with the state on your behalf. You receive the complete packet in filing order and file it as the applicant. Licensing and enrollment approvals belong to the state and the payer, and no builder can promise them; what I promise is a packet assembled from the state's own published requirements, with the sources shown.
