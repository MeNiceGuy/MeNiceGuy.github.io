Home Care Licensing by State

Every state runs home care licensing its own way: a different agency, a different fee, a different order of operations. These guides walk each state's process in the order the state runs it, with the fees and timelines tied to the state's own published sources, and the walkthrough video for the state on the same page. I build them from the same verified research that backs the state packs in the store and the Manual and Template Generator linked below.

Starting a Home Care Agency in VirginiaThe Home Care Organization license: the $1,500 triennial fee, the 60-day application rule, reserve funds, insurance, and the prelicensure survey.Starting a Home Care Agency in North CarolinaThe DHSR license in the state's order: the $510 fee, the approved-training gate, the desk review, and the December 31 renewal cliff.Starting a Home Care Agency in MarylandThe Residential Service Agency license from OHCQ under COMAR 10.07.05: the five required attachments, the fee the rulebook lists, and the 3-year term.Starting a Home Care Agency in TexasThe HCSSA license and the PAS category: the $2,625 fee, TULIP, administrator training before designation, and the six-month survey clock.Starting a Home Care Agency in OhioThe Nonmedical Home Health Services license under R.C. Chapter 3740: the $250 fee paid by cashier's check or money order, the $20,000 surety bond, and owner fingerprinting.Starting a Home Care Agency in GeorgiaThe Private Home Care Provider license under Rule 111-8-65: $300 plus $250 per service category, the 15-document GAHLES packet, and the provisional clock.Starting a Home Care Agency in IndianaThe Personal Services Agency license from the Department of Health under IC 16-27: the $250 nonrefundable fee, and why the license comes before the first client.Starting a Home Care Agency in MichiganNo state license for non-medical home care: the business setup that still applies, and how to open to a standard referral sources respect.Starting a Home Care Agency in FloridaCompanion and homemaker services register with AHCA under section 400.509: the $50.75 two-year registration, Level 2 screening, and the 60-day decision clock.Starting a Home Care Agency in PennsylvaniaThe agency and registry license under 28 Pa. Code Chapter 611: the $100 fee, one license per location, the hiring rules, and the self-built competency program.
