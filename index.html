How I Work

Written. Asynchronous. Verified.

"All engagements are delivered in writing on a fully asynchronous model: the client sends materials and data, the firm returns written findings with implementation guides, keeping scope and the record exact." From the firm's capability statement
  • You work with the principalI am a solo consultant. There is no account team and no handoff. The person who diagnoses the problem is the person who writes the findings.
  • Every claim is tracedEach engagement ships with a verification file that ties every recommendation to its source regulation or policy, so you can cross-check the work yourself.
  • Numbers firstThe diagnosis starts with your data, stated plainly: what needs focus, what is improving, what is declining.
  • The record stays exactBecause everything is in writing, scope never drifts and nothing depends on what was said on a call.

The Async Advantage

Why owners choose an asynchronous consultant

  • Nothing gets lostEvery question, finding, and decision lives in writing. Nothing depends on what somebody remembers from a call.
  • No calendar tagNo scheduling, no time zones, no rescheduling. You send materials when you have them, and the work moves from there.
  • Checked answers, not fast onesYour questions get researched and verified before they are answered, not handled off the cuff in a live meeting.
  • Your hours buy workTime goes into findings and documents, not into meetings about the work.
  • Scope cannot driftThe engagement is defined in writing at the start, so what you asked for is exactly what gets delivered.
  • Easy to shareWritten findings forward cleanly to a partner, a lender, or your team, word for word as delivered.
  • Guides, not remindersEvery fix ships with step-by-step how-to guides, so your team can run it without me. The important information stays on hand, and nobody has to chase anybody for a reminder.
  • No fee, no waitingI review the problem in writing first, free. If I can fix it, the work comes back in days, not weeks, and you pay for the fix, never for a conversation about it.

Training repayment agreement voided

Section 504 accountability

A Virginia school division was not implementing a student's Section 504 plan. I documented the failures, engaged division administrators, and filed a federal complaint with the U.S. Department of Education, Office for Civil Rights (No. 11-25-1139). Case restored

Virginia, Veteran Directed Care

Program budget audit

Caregiver pay in a Veteran Directed Care case did not match submitted timesheets, the budget showed a negative balance nobody could explain, and agency records contradicted the approved schedule in the participant portal. I audited the portal against the agency's records, diagnosed the budget mismanagement, and escalated in writing to the agency's president and chief executive officer. The agency ordered a leadership review, and its quality assurance specialist took over the case, investigated the findings, corrected the case, and restored it to good standing.
