I build the packet. You file it.

If you would rather not build the packet yourself, I build the complete licensing packet for you, delivered in filing order, ready for your review and submission.

Every packet follows the same written process. You tell me your agency type and your state, I build the packet in filing order, and you receive the finished documents with a cover note that explains what goes where. No calls, no meetings, no back-and-forth that drags on for weeks.

Billing services

Non-medical Medicaid and waiver billing, handled by email and portals.

Billing breaks down in small places: a missed EVV match, a code that does not fit the waiver, a claim filed one step out of order. I handle non-medical Medicaid and waiver billing for the agencies I work with, and I keep the process written and traceable.

Everything runs through email and the payer portals. You send what I need, I work the billing, and you get a written record of what was filed and what is still open.
